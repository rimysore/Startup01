## 1. Y Combinator

- W27 (Winter 2027) is the open batch. On-time deadline **2 Nov 2026, 8pm PT**; on-time applicants get a decision by **11 Dec 2026**; late applications still considered with no promised date. Batch runs **Jan to Mar 2027 in San Francisco**. [YC Apply](https://www.ycombinator.com/apply) (read 2026-10-21)
- Future batches (Spring, Summer, Fall) can be applied to now via "Early Decision": choose "A batch after Winter 2027" and name the batch. No dates are published for them. [YC Early Decision](https://www.ycombinator.com/early-decision) (read 2026-10-21)
- Spring batch letter is now **P** (Primavera), not X; X25 was renamed P25 and X26 became P26. So Spring 2027 = **P27**, not X27. Projected P27 deadline is ~Feb 2027 with the batch running Apr to Jun 2027. This is a third-party projection, not an official YC date; P26's deadline was 9 Feb 2026. [zyner.io YC deadlines](https://zyner.io/blog/yc-application-deadline) (states "as of 9 Sep 2026")
- YC runs four batches a year (Winter, Spring, Summer, Fall). [Indian Express, 18 Apr 2026](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/bangalore/y-combinator-startup-school-india-bengaluru-10643416/)
- **Standard deal:** $500k in total, as two SAFEs signed at the same time: $125k on a post-money SAFE for 7%, plus $375k on an uncapped SAFE with MFN (it converts at the lowest-cap SAFE issued before the priced round). Example given: at a $15M cap the MFN SAFE converts to about 2.5%. YC also gets pro rata rights. No fees, no milestones. [YC Deal](https://www.ycombinator.com/deal) (read 2026-10-21)
- **Entity:** you don't need to incorporate before applying. To take part, a non-US company must create a parent in the **US, Canada, Singapore or the Cayman Islands**, and YC helps with this after acceptance. The FAQ doesn't mention Delaware or India. [YC FAQ](https://www.ycombinator.com/faq) (read 2026-10-21). A secondary source restates this: "YC invests in US, Canadian, Cayman and Singapore corporations. Companies incorporated elsewhere must set up a parent." [TrustList, 28 Sep 2026](https://trustlist.uk/news/y-combinator-winter-2027-deadline-2-november-techstars-spring-2027-deadline-18-november)
- **An Indian Pvt Ltd on its own is not accepted.** It would become a subsidiary of a US/SG/CA/Cayman parent (a "flip"). ET names this parent-entity requirement as a key reason Indian YC selections fell. [ET, "From 66 to 4"](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/startups/y-combinators-india-cohort-shrinks-amid-local-capital-reshaping-deals-ai-focus/articleshow/122326955.cms) (2025, exact date not shown)
- **Cost of reversing a flip later:** Groww paid about $159.4M (about ₹1,340 cr) in US exit taxes to reverse-flip to India before its IPO. Razorpay's cost is estimated at about $200M, and Meesho's was expected at $280-300M. [ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/startups/y-combinators-india-cohort-shrinks-amid-local-capital-reshaping-deals-ai-focus/articleshow/122326955.cms); [Treelife case study](https://treelife.in/case-studies/how-growws-160-million-delaware-tax-bill-became-indias-most-expensive-startup-lesson/) (date not shown)
- **International founders:** "open to any startup, anywhere in the world"; YC's immigration attorneys help founders get US visas. [YC FAQ](https://www.ycombinator.com/faq) (read 2026-10-21)
- **In person:** "The batch takes place in-person in San Francisco. It kicks off with a 3-day, in-person retreat." After the 3 months founders can go anywhere. [YC FAQ](https://www.ycombinator.com/faq). In 2023 YC said "We ask all our founders to move to the Bay Area for the 3 months of YC." [Inc42, 19 Sep 2023](https://inc42.com/features/startup-accelerator-y-combinator-indian-startup/)
- **Part-time founders:** you may apply while employed or studying, but "we expect the founders to commit to working full-time on their company during the batch and afterwards if accepted." [YC FAQ](https://www.ycombinator.com/faq)
- **Idea stage is fine:** "On average, 40% of the companies we fund in each batch are just an idea"; "7% of recent batches had more than $50k in monthly revenue when accepted." [YC FAQ](https://www.ycombinator.com/faq)
- **India trend:** YC picked 4 Indian startups in 2024, down from 66 in 2021. Reasons given: the parent-entity requirement, reverse-flip taxes, and YC's AI/deeptech focus. [ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/startups/y-combinators-india-cohort-shrinks-amid-local-capital-reshaping-deals-ai-focus/articleshow/122326955.cms). Of 213 India-linked YC startups since 2012, about 20 reached Series A or later. [Mint, 26 Sep 2025](https://www.livemint.com/companies/y-combinator-startup-accelerator-vc-funding-unicorn-11758786539408.html). In 2025 the only Asian startup in YC was Bolna, a voice AI company. [Asia Tech Review, 6 Feb 2026](https://www.asiatechreview.com/p/y-combinator-reaps-big-rewards-in)
- YC still says it wants Indian founders. Partner Ankit Gupta: "for companies that want to be based in India, sell to the Indian market, and IPO in India, we're happy to support them too." [ET, 13 Apr 2026](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/startups/india-has-strong-pipeline-of-ideas-keen-to-back-local-founders-y-combinators-ankit-gupta/articleshow/130236687.cms). YC ran its first **Startup School India** in Bengaluru on 18 Apr 2026: 25,000+ applications, about 2,250 attendees. [SKIL Events, 28 Apr 2026](https://www.skilevents.com/insights/news/skil-events-y-combinator-startup-school-india-bengaluru). Whether a 2027 edition is planned: not found.
- **Recent India-located YC companies** (YC directory, read 2026-10-21; 9 were visible): F25 Kailash Labs (video models), Perseus (coding-agent context), Redapto (AI ops agents), Bolna AI (voice AI); W26 Cardboard (agentic video editor), Ressl AI (agent training); P26 BentoLabs AI (agent monitoring), Lab0 (enterprise implementation); S26 Waybill (hardware procurement). **None are SMB, creator-tools or local-commerce companies**; all are AI/agent/dev or B2B infra. [YC companies: India](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/location/india)
- AI share of YC batches: 76% in the 2025 batch per a LinkedIn post (secondary source, low reliability). [LinkedIn, 1 Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rajnandani-jain_yc-activity-7478002028342829056-o6q_). More than 70% of YC's 2023 intake was AI. [Asia Tech Review](https://www.asiatechreview.com/p/y-combinator-reaps-big-rewards-in)

## 2. Antler India

- Deal: "Our standard deal is up to ~$425K (~INR 4 crores) per company for 11% equity", with up to $30M potential follow-on. Antler aims to invest in 50-75% of Residency teams. One-time ₹2L grant, office space in Bengaluru, $1M+ AI perks ($350K guaranteed Azure credits), up to $4M perks after investment. [Antler India page](https://www.antler.co/location/india) (read 2026-10-21); [Antler AI Residency blog, 4 Feb 2026](https://www.antler.co/blog/introducing-antlers-ai-residency)
- The earlier India Residency cheque was about half that. The ₹4 Cr is "double of our historical India Residency cheque" (Nitin Sharma, Antler India). [LinkedIn, 4 Feb 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nitinsharma15_presenting-the-new-ai-residency-ai-leaders-activity-7424724873849036800-Fk15). A third-party listing cites ₹2 Cr for 9% for older cohorts. [startupgrantsindia](https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/providers/antler-india) (date not shown)
- **Structure:** 3-week fast-track with 2 weeks in person in Bengaluru, then weekly GP office hours; investment decision in about 3 weeks. Teams must be "pre-launch, pre-product, or pre-revenue", committed full-time and "available to join full-time in Bangalore". [Antler India](https://www.antler.co/location/india)
- **2026 cohorts:** 2nd AI Residency applications opened 4 Feb 2026, deadline 19 Feb 2026, Residency started 23 Feb 2026, aiming for 12 teams. The first AI Residency had 2,600 applicants, about 1% selected, and 6 ventures funded. [Antler blog](https://www.antler.co/blog/introducing-antlers-ai-residency); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nitinsharma15_presenting-the-new-ai-residency-ai-leaders-activity-7424724873849036800-Fk15)
- Antler says it moved to a **new cohort every quarter on a 45-day cycle**, with smaller cohorts. [YourStory, 29 Jan 2026](https://yourstory.com/2026/01/antler-doubling-checks-speed-amidst-flurry-ai-companies-india). In Jul 2026 it showcased 9 startups, including **Kenko AI, which "automates administrative work for small service businesses"**; it has backed 115+ startups from its $75M India fund. [YourStory, 28 Jul 2026](https://yourstory.com/2026/07/antler-india-bets-on-ai-climate-deeptech-with-new-startup-cohort)
- **Next residency dates:** not found. The Antler India page says "Next Residency will be announced soon" (read 2026-10-21). The cohort-dates page has no India entry; the US (SF/Austin/NYC) is rolling. [Antler cohort dates](https://www.antler.co/cohort-start-dates)
- Focus is "AI-first", but Antler says it welcomes exceptional companies outside AI. [Antler India](https://www.antler.co/location/india)

## 3. 100X.VC

- Invests **₹1.25 crore per startup** using **iSAFE** notes (India SAFE). [100X.VC Class 12 press release, 18 Jan 2025](https://www.100x.vc/press-releases/100-x-vc-invests-usd-2-7-mn-in-18-startups-as-part-of-class-12). Older press coverage gives the ₹1.25 cr as being for 15% of future equity. [WebSearch summary of 100x.vc/apply](https://www.100x.vc/apply); that page returned 404 on 2026-10-21, so the 15% figure is **unverified**.
- iSAFE templates come in three forms: "Valuation Cap with Discount", "Valuation Cap" and "Discount". The amount is agreed with each startup. [100X.VC iSAFE](https://www.100x.vc/isafe) (read 2026-10-21)
- **Class model:** it invests before each cohort formally starts, then mentors for 100+ hours, then holds a VC Pitch Day in Mumbai. Class 12 picked 18 startups from 304 shortlisted out of 1,900+ applicants (about 0.9% acceptance), with Pitch Day on 18 Jan 2025. Total of 180 investments since Jul 2019. [100X.VC](https://www.100x.vc/press-releases/100-x-vc-invests-usd-2-7-mn-in-18-startups-as-part-of-class-12)
- Applications are rolling. Shortlisted startups are invited to Mumbai for an interview, and a successful interview leads to a term sheet (WebSearch summary of 100x.vc/apply, page now 404). A third-party listing says applications are currently closed: [IncorpX](https://www.incorpx.io/grants/100x-vc-investment-program) (date not shown).
- **2026-27 class schedule: not found.** No Class 13/14 announcement was found. PitchBook shows 100X.VC deals dated 6 Oct 2026 (Sagepilot), 15 Sep 2026 (CopperPilot) and 19 Aug 2026 (DIAGNA). [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/investor/277319-62) (read 2026-10-21)
- Two co-founders (Yagnesh Sanghrajka, Vatsal Kanakiya) left in late 2024 and started 247VC. [VCCircle tag page](https://www.vccircle.com/tag/100xvc) (items dated 29 Apr and 20 May 2025)

## 5. Angel networks

- **Indian Angel Network (IAN Group):** "273+ Companies backed", ₹1,500+ Cr invested; IAN Alpha Fund closed at $100M. IAN describes itself as "an introducer between the investor and start-up companies". Startups apply through "Submit Plan". Fees to startups are **not stated** on the site. [iangroup.vc](https://iangroup.vc/) (read 2026-10-21). IAN's second fund had a first close of ₹355 Cr toward a ₹1,000 Cr target. [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=429862) (date not shown). IAN once ran a small-ticket program of ₹25L at a fixed ₹2.5 Cr post-money. [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=19568) (old, about 2017; may no longer run). A secondary blog estimates ₹25L to ₹2 Cr per round and an 8-16 week process: [GrowSapph](https://www.growsapph.com/blog/angel-investing-platforms-india-comparison-2026) (low reliability).
- **Inflection Point Ventures (IPV):** **charges startups** "a mutually agreed upon % of total amount raised via IPV", discussed after the voting stage. [IPV startup FAQ](https://ipventures.in/startups-faqs/) (read 2026-10-21). On the investor side: annual fee ₹33,800+GST and a 2% investment documentation fee. [IPV investor FAQ](https://ipventures.in/investors-faqs/). It is sector-agnostic, pre-seed to pre-Series A. Example: led Pinq Polka's ₹4 Cr pre-Series A (Mar 2026). [WebSearch aggregate of CB Insights/Seedtable](https://www.cbinsights.com/investor/inflection-point-ventures-1) (low reliability). IPV's own cheque size: not found.
- **Venture Catalysts (VCats):** invests "₹2 crore to ₹15 crore per start-up through a network of HNIs, Family Offices, CXOs" for **5-15% stake**. [The Hindu, 2 Nov 2024](https://www.thehindu.com/business/early-stage-investor-venture-catalysts-looks-at-investing-in-chip-space-tech-ai-start-ups/article68820170.ece). Its accelerator fund 9Unicorns offers $100k for 5-7% equity. [Inc42, 1 Sep 2020](https://inc42.com/buzz/startup-incubator-venture-catalysts-announces-first-close-of-inr-300-cr-fund-9unicorns/). Investors pay a 2.3% transaction fee plus ₹45k+GST a year. [VCats FAQ on Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/981896732/VCats-FAQs) (date not shown). **Startup-side fee: not found.** Founders accused it of walking back term sheets in 2022. [The Morning Context, 22 Sep 2022](https://themorningcontext.com/internet/founders-accuse-venture-catalysts-of-ghosting)
- **LetsVenture / LVX:** "LetsVenture charges a fee to the Startup based on the funds being raised on the platform and upto 5% carry from Investors"; startups sign up free and the fee is charged only on close. [LVX FAQ](https://lvxventures.com/faq) (read 2026-10-21). The fee was historically 2% of funds raised. [Inc42, 19 Oct 2015](https://inc42.com/flash-feed/letsventure-series-a/). Startups can raise up to ₹10 Cr from angels on the platform. [LV equity FAQ](https://letsventure-40b95388f1f4749efaade03d810.webflow.io/equity-faq)
- Market context: angels deployed about $1.7B across about 3.4K deals in 2020-25. [Eximius Pre-seed Playbook 2026 PDF](https://eximiusvc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Investment-report_Designed-version_V-Final-18Feb-1.pdf) (Feb/Mar 2026). India Quotient's Anand Lunia: "angel syndicates are fewer and smaller firms are tighter". [ET, H1 2026](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/startups/startup-seed-rounds-double-as-vcs-turn-more-selective/articleshow/132023936.cms)
- DPIIT recognition is described as "a prerequisite for most angel networks, government schemes". [IncorpX, 8 Aug 2026](https://www.incorpx.io/blog/pre-seed-seed-funding-2026-raise-first-round) (secondary). This needs an Indian Pvt Ltd/LLP.

### VC stance and thresholds
- **Blume:** "We, for example, have very little appetite for narrowly defined SMB products or enterprise software catering only to an Indian market." For first-time founders it typically does not do idea-stage or pre-MVP. Reach-out benchmarks: B2B or transaction plays at a **$375k (₹3 cr) annualised revenue rate**, marketplaces at **₹50 lakh monthly GMV**, unmonetised consumer apps at **25k MAU / 5k DAU**. It invests in 10-12 of 4,000-5,000 pitches a year, mostly referrals. [blume.vc FAQ](https://blume.vc/) (read 2026-10-21)
- **Micro-VC norms 2026 (Blume):** micro VCs (<$40M corpus) "typically lead with $500K-$800K cheques and take an 8-12% stake"; $40-100M funds lead with $1-1.5M for 12-15%. Titan Capital, Better Capital, Whiteboard and Sparrow are listed as sector-agnostic; Java Capital as defence/deeptech. [Blume, 31 Mar 2026](https://blume.vc/commentaries/indias-micro-vc-landscape-whos-writing-the-first-cheque-in-2026)
- **First Cheque (India Quotient):** invests up to $500k at paper-plan, pre-product or MVP stage; first call to term sheet in 10 days; plans 15-20 companies over 18 months; "The best time to pitch is when you haven't got a single customer." [firstcheque.vc](https://www.firstcheque.vc/) (read 2026-10-21)
- Cheque ranges from a secondary source (low reliability): Better Capital $100K-$2M; Titan Capital $100K-$1.5M; Whiteboard $300K-$1.5M. [VC Dekho, 10 Jun 2026](https://vcdekho.com/blog/micro-vcs-india-first-cheque)

### Accelerator programs and windows
- **Peak XV Surge:** invests $500k to $5M (ceiling raised from $3M); applications are rolling and reviewed in 2-3 weeks; it works with idea-stage founders. Surge 12 (18 startups, >$50M invested) was unveiled 28 Sep 2026, with only 5 of 18 focused on the Indian market. The next cohort was said to kick off Sept 2026 (that is Surge 12), with 8 weeks ending in an SF immersion. The next cohort after that is not announced. [Surge FAQ](https://surge.peakxv.com/faqs) (read 2026-10-21); [TechCrunch, 28 Sep 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/peak-xv-goes-bigger-at-seed-with-new-surge-cohort-as-series-a-bar-rises/)
- **Accel Atoms:** pre-seed program for Indian and Indian-origin founders anywhere. It invests up to $1M-$2M as equity or a convertible note, for companies that have raised under $2M. Incorporation is not needed to apply but is required on selection. Applications are rolling for the AI and X cohorts. ## Things that hurt Mitra's chances (synthesis; my analysis, not sourced facts)
1. **Part-time co-founder:** YC requires full-time founders during and after the batch; Antler requires full-time presence in Bangalore.
2. **Split geography (builder in the US, marketer in India):** YC needs both in SF for 3 months (the Indian founder needs a visa, which YC helps with); Antler needs both in Bengaluru; 100X.VC interviews in Mumbai.
3. **No entity:** YC needs a US/SG/CA/Cayman parent (a Delaware C-corp is the natural fit given the US founder), but Indian angel networks and DPIIT benefits want an Indian Pvt Ltd. A US parent with an Indian subsidiary (flip) suits YC and US money, but a later reverse flip is expensive (Groww ~$160M).
4. **Not AI-first:** YC (~70%+ AI), Google GFSA (AI-first plus traction required), Accel Atoms AI (Gemini integration) and Antler's AI Residency all skew AI. Mitra's scam-checking could be framed as AI, but it is not core AI or infra.
5. **India-only SMB SaaS at ₹499-1,499/month:** Blume explicitly has "very little appetite for narrowly defined SMB products... catering only to an Indian market"; Surge 12 had only 5 of 18 India-market companies.
6. **Zero traction while seed deal count halved** (Tracxn: 420 seed rounds in H1 2026 vs 797). Investors now want paying pilots even at pre-seed.
7. **Crowded and funded competitors:** Grexa AI (21.6k SMBs incl. gyms, salons, cafés), Zoca (Accel), Influish (5-10 lakh creators, profitable), Creedom, Creonnect, TIST.
8. **Positives:** Bengaluru base, Kannada/regional angle (STAGE-style regional thesis backed by Blume), idea-stage-friendly cheques (First Cheque, 100X.VC, Better Capital, Antler, Surge), and YC's stated openness to India-based companies.
