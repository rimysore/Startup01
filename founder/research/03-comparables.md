# 03. Business type and comparable companies (22 Oct 2026)

Figures are as publicly reported, with links and dates. "Not reported" means I found no public figure. Several numbers come from a single secondary source; check the primary page before quoting one externally. Company-reported metrics are labelled as such.

## 1. What kind of company Mitra is

Mitra is **vertical SaaS plus a two-sided marketplace plus embedded payments, for local SMBs**, with a free creator tool as the supply-side hook.

| Layer | Mitra's version | Closest public analogues |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free supply hook | Offer inbox, scam check, media kit for creators | Namma Yatri's zero-commission app for drivers; Meesho's free reseller tools |
| SMB SaaS (what pays) | Track: coupon and counter redeem, cost per customer, monthly plan | Petpooja (restaurant POS), Toast, Yelp/Nextdoor self-serve ads, **TryNearby** |
| Marketplace | Matching businesses with nearby creators; base fee plus customer bonus | TryNearby, Popular Pays, Mustard, Crafted (Bengaluru) |
| Payments (later) | Split payouts via Razorpay Route or Cashfree | Toast (fintech ≈ most revenue), Mavely (pays creators per sale), Fivestars → SumUp |
| Long-term | Local performance-ads network plus creator payments rail | Google Local Services Ads (pay per verified lead), DoorDash ads, magicpin |

**What investors will compare it to first: TryNearby (YC Summer 2026, Los Angeles).** Restaurants pay **$399/month** and get a steady stream of nearby creators who visit, film and post. AI agents handle matching and booking. It reports **120+ paying restaurants, 2,500+ videos, ~35M views, 37% month-on-month growth and over 90% monthly restaurant retention** (all company-reported, [YC page](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/trynearby), read 22 Oct 2026). That is the same thesis in the US at roughly **70× Mitra's planned ₹499 price** (judgement: $399 ≈ ₹35,000 at an assumed ~₹88 per dollar; check the current rate). Use it as proof the model works and as the benchmark to beat in India. Don't hide it.

## 4. Six lessons for Mitra, in order of how much they matter

1. **Ask for money early, tied to an outcome.** Khatabook, OkCredit and Dukaan show that millions of free Indian merchants produced little revenue. Keep Track free only up to a point and charge once customers are counted. The `BUSINESS_MODEL.md` upgrade prompt is right; switch it on in the pilot, not after.
2. **The price is the hardest problem, not the product.** TryNearby charges ~$399/month. At ₹499, Mitra needs ~70 times as many businesses for the same revenue (judgement), and Dukaan shows micro-merchants can't carry a sales team. Test a higher tier (₹2,000–5,000 a month for "4 creator visits a month, done for you") with 5 venues alongside ₹499.
3. **Services may beat cafés.** Groupon (salons best, restaurants worst) and Yelp (services up, restaurants down) point the same way. Keep food for density, but put salons, gyms and clinics into the pilot.
4. **Keep the discount shallow and report repeat customers.** Groupon's merchants lost money on deep discounts with ~20% return. Default the coupon to 5–10%, and show "new vs returning" in results.
5. **Creators must earn from business budgets, per result.** Moj's subsidised pay failed; Mavely's pay-per-sale sold for $250M. The base fee plus per-customer bonus design is right. Never fund creator pay from Mitra's own money.
6. **Integrate, don't replace.** Petpooja runs restaurant billing, and WhatsApp's Meta Business Agent now books appointments (`05-timing.md`). Redeeming at billing and reporting on WhatsApp will spread faster than a separate counter app.
