## The 10 most important findings

1. **The model is validated, but not by Mitra.** TryNearby (YC Summer 2026, LA) charges restaurants **$399/month** for local creator visits and reports **120+ paying restaurants, >90% monthly retention and 37% MoM growth** (company-reported, [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/trynearby)). That is proof the idea works, and it is the bar investors will hold Mitra to.
2. **Mitra has a strong prototype and no market proof yet.** It has 0 businesses, 0 counted customers and 0 paying users. All buy rates so far come from simulated panels. The founders' own board scores it 5.0/10 for exactly this reason.
3. **Indian SMBs rarely pay for free tools.** Khatabook (8M+ merchants) made ₹102.7 cr in FY24 against a ₹116 cr loss. OkCredit made ₹23.3 cr in FY25 on $83M raised. Dukaan dropped micro-merchants. At ₹499/month, Mitra needs ~70× TryNearby's venue count for the same revenue. **Price and the cost of signing up a business are the real risks**, not the product.
4. **The team setup is the biggest fixable negative.** YC requires founders to be full-time during and after the batch. Antler India requires full-time founders in Bengaluru. Today one founder is in the USA and one is part-time.
5. **Indian grants are mostly closed to you for now.** The Startup India Seed Fund closed to applicants on **31 May 2026**, with no successor found. The ELEVATE general call closed on **15 Jul 2026**; the next one is likely mid-2027 and needs a Karnataka entity. FoF money only comes through VCs. Fix the old dates in `vision/PLAN-marketplace.md`.
6. **YC W27 closes on 2 Nov 2026 at 8pm PT; decisions come by 11 Dec.** You don't need a company to apply. YC invests only in a US, Canada, Singapore or Cayman parent. India's YC count fell from 66 (2021) to 4 (2024), partly because of the parent rule and the tax cost of moving back to India later.
7. **Entity: an Indian Pvt Ltd in Karnataka now; flip only if YC or a US lead requires it, and immediately while the value is near zero** (judgement). Two reasons. Every Indian benefit needs an Indian company. And a Delaware parent with an Indian subsidiary triggers an ODI rule: an India-resident founder with ≥10% may need approval. Reverse flips cost Groww ~₹1,340 cr and Razorpay ~₹1,245 cr.
8. **Never hold the money.** Under RBI's 2025 payment-aggregator rules, a payment aggregator needs ₹15 cr net worth and may not run a marketplace. Use Razorpay Route or Cashfree Easy Split. Money flowing through Mitra probably makes it an e-commerce operator, which means mandatory GST registration, 0.5% TCS and 0.1% TDS (old 194-O, now s.393). Ask both providers whether a pre-revenue company qualifies.
9. **Meta has made discovery and messaging free.** The Creator Marketing Hub went live in Sep 2026, the WhatsApp Business Agent (which books appointments) launched in India on 8 Jul 2026, and Reels gained Kannada AI translation in Nov 2025. What nobody owns yet is **customers counted at the counter per creator post, in dense Kannada neighbourhoods**, plus a creator scam check. Zomato District and magicpin could add creator codes, and that is the biggest latent threat.
10. **The name "Mitra" is crowded.** Vahan, a YC-backed Bengaluru startup, runs a WhatsApp job bot called **Mitra** and owns mitra.app, and Airtel, Fino and SBI MF have "Mitra" apps. File a distinctive compound mark plus the logo in Classes 9, 35 and 42 (₹4,500 per class to e-file as a startup). Separately, **trymitraapp.com still shows the old YouTube analytics product.**
