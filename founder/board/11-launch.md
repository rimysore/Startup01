# Launch Lead
Score: 3/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## The cheapest real test and its pass/fail line
Don't build a marketplace. Skills are free markdown files ([767 installs across 162 skills on one publisher page](https://www.skills.sh/jarmen423)). Test whether a finished deliverable sells.

**Segment:** solo founders and small businesses who would otherwise post a $100-400 Fiverr gig. Developers install skills free, so they are the wrong buyer.

**The test (Wizard of Oz):** one page, "Hire an AI specialist," three fixed-price offers, paid up front through Stripe Payment Links, delivered within 48h:
1. Landing-page UX audit plus redesigned hero (ui-ux-pro-max): $149
2. Messy spreadsheet to clean model with dashboard (xlsx): $99
3. Security audit of a small repo, with fixes as a PR (security skill): $249

The founder runs Claude Code with the skill behind the page and checks every output by hand. Money-back guarantee. No catalog, no supply side. Cost: about $1,500 in ads.

**Pass/fail, 14 days, strangers only (friends don't count):**
- PASS: at least 15 paid orders, average at least $120, refunds at most 20%, founder time at most 45 min per order, at least 3 repeat buyers or requests for a subscription.
- ITERATE ONCE: 5-14 orders. Change the offer or price and rerun for 7 days, with the same bar.
- KILL: fewer than 5 paid orders, or more than 90 min of hand-fixing per order (that's freelancing with an AI label).

## First 30 days (10-27 to 11-25)
Weekly: visitor-to-paid (target at least 2%), CAC under 50% of average order, founder minutes per order, refunds, repeats, winning offer.
- **Day 7 (11-03):** One offer over 60% of orders? Drop the others. CAC over $150? Kill ads, go outbound.
- **Day 14 (11-10):** Pass/kill decision. If pass, raise prices 30% to find the ceiling.
- **Day 30 (11-25):** Can the winner be 80% automated? Sell 5 buyers a $299-499/mo retainer. Only then write platform code.

## Timing: too late?
For a horizontal "GPT Store for skills," yes:
- [Anthropic's marketplace](https://www.letsdatascience.com/news/anthropic-launches-claude-marketplace-for-enterprises-36871422), Claude Code plugins, skills.sh and SkillsMP already distribute skills free.
- The GPT Store showed builder payouts don't happen ([Slashdot/Wired](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/24/10/11/1033247/openais-gpt-store-has-left-some-developers-in-the-lurch)).
- Upwork has shipped [ChatGPT/Claude apps and an MCP server](https://snipework.com/news/upwork-mcp-server-ai-integration) and names ["launching agents in our marketplace"](https://www.insidermonkey.com/blog/upwork-inc-nasdaqupwk-q1-2026-earnings-call-transcript-1760949/) as its next lever, with 785k clients.

Not too late for done-for-you outcomes for non-technical buyers, until Upwork ships agents (my guess: 6-12 months).

## What would kill it (top 3)
1. **No moat on supply.** Skills are copyable; any Fiverr freelancer runs Claude with the same skill (Fiverr reports [AI-agent searches up 18,347%](https://block.fiverr.com/news/spring-bti-2025), all for human sellers).
2. **Platform absorption.** Agent.ai was [folded into HubSpot](https://www.startuphub.ai/news/hubspot-absorbs-agent-ai-into-crm-core); you'd rent distribution from Anthropic, OpenAI or Upwork.
3. **QA cost and liability.** About 12-13% of public skills are [malicious or critically flawed](https://snyk.io/blog/toxicskills-malicious-ai-agent-skills-clawhub). Vetting and hand-fixing eat the margin on $99-249 tickets.

## If FUND IF: conditions
1. The 14-day test above passes, with strangers and real money.
2. Narrow to the single winning deliverable and sell the outcome, not the catalog.
3. Do not build a two-sided marketplace or author revenue share until there is $10k MRR from that one offer.
4. Keep founder minutes per order under 20 by day 60. If not, it's an agency, not a company.

## Market ceiling
A horizontal skill-agent marketplace is a **million-dollar** business at best: low tens of millions ARR before a platform absorbs it, because the platforms own the buyers and give distribution away. The [$4.6T service-as-software pool](https://foundationcapital.com/ai-service-as-software/) is real, but Sierra- and Harvey-scale companies capture it by owning one vertical outcome. **Billion** only if the test reveals one job worth going deep on, which would be a different company.
