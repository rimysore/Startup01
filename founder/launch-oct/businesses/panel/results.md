# Buyer panel: Mitra (beta), creators and a customer counter for Bengaluru local businesses

**11 buy · 9 pass** (55% buy) out of 20 simulated buyers. Seed 571712, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## Why they pass

| reason | buyers | in their words |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| habit | 4 | "My agency already handles Instagram and picks the creators, and I already have creators asking me for free food every week, so finding more is not my problem. I'm not going to get staff at several outlets learning a coupon-and-PIN routine for a beta app from a small team." (P001) · "My salon runs on regulars, Google reviews and the WhatsApp group, and that keeps my chairs full. I've never used creators and I don't see why I'd start just because the app is free." (P010) |
| trust | 3 | "I already paid a creator once and got likes, not customers, so I'm not rushing back into creators just because the app is free. A beta from a small team I've never heard of could vanish next month, and my staff would have to learn a PIN system for nothing." (P008) · "I don't know who you are, and a beta with a small team could shut down next month. On top of that it's another app with a Google login and PINs for my staff, and my nephew already handles the Instagram side, so I'd rather leave it." (P014) |
| convenience | 2 | "Counting customers per creator is exactly what I needed last time I got burned, but I don't have time for another app and I don't want my staff fumbling with coupon links and PINs at a busy front desk. Free doesn't help if it costs me attention and still means paying creators who might bring nobody." (P009) · "It is free, but free still costs me time. Google sign-in, a new app, coupon links and teaching my girls a PIN on a busy Saturday is exactly the kind of thing I avoid; I just answer the creators on WhatsApp or ignore them." (P012) |

## Why they buy

| reason | buyers | in their words |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| need | 11 | "It's free and I already get 3-4 creator DMs a week with no idea if any of them bring anyone in. I'll add the café and run one barter post through it to see if the numbers match my book." (P002) · "I already give free meals to creators every month and have no idea if anyone walks in because of them, so a free tool that counts customers per creator is worth a 2-minute sign-up. I'd try it on one or two barter posts at the quietest outlet first." (P003) |

## What would flip a no

- If my agency used it and showed me a month of real numbers, customers per creator from one of my own outlets, without me or my staff doing extra work.
- Seeing another café owner near me show real redeemed coupons and actual walk-in customers from a creator post through Mitra.
- Seeing another salon or gym near me show real customer counts from a creator post, with setup done for me so my staff only has to tap redeem.
- Another salon owner I know in my area telling me it actually brought them paying customers who came back, with the numbers to show it.
- Another darshini owner near me showing me their redeemed-coupon sheet matched to their bill book, with real new customers from one barter post.
- If someone from Mitra came to the salon, set it up for me and it worked over WhatsApp with no new app or login for me or my staff.
- Another salon or gym owner I know in my area telling me it actually brought them customers, and someone from your team coming to the shop to set it up so it works over WhatsApp without me learning a new app.
- Another café or darshini owner near me telling me in person that a creator post through Mitra brought them real customers they could match in their bill book.
- If it worked fully on WhatsApp with no app or login, and someone from Mitra came to my shop, set it up and showed me a nearby darshini that actually got counted customers from it.

Buyers say they would buy **1.1 times** in the first month on average.
