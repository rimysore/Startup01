Mitra (beta): find local creators who fit your business, and count the customers each post brings. Bengaluru, Kannada and English.
Add your business once (Google sign-in, 2 minutes). Creators near you see it straight away; you see who is interested and which creators fit your area, with their verified Instagram followers and average views.
Every creator post gets a coupon link. Customers show it on their phone; your staff tap redeem with a 4-digit PIN. You see customers per creator and cost per customer, paid or barter, and can download a daily sheet to check against your bill book.
Free for businesses and brands. Mitra takes no cut of what you pay creators.
Beta: a small team, Bengaluru only for now.
