# Marketing: Mitra (beta), October 2026

Evidence used: `board.md` (3 fund-if, 4.0/10), the two simulated panels in `creators/panel/results.md` (6 buy · 14 pass) and `businesses/panel/results.md` (11 buy · 9 pass), the Influish facts in `board/brief.md`. Panels are simulated: their quotes below are research, never ad copy, and the buy rates are an upper bound.

**What this plan changes from the pitch.** The board asked for one thing to lead with. Both panels and all three board members point to the same one: **the customer counter**. So the order of the campaign is businesses first (they say yes more, 55% vs 30%, and they fill the room creators are afraid is empty), then creators, who are shown real businesses and real counted customers rather than a promise.

---

## 1. Positioning

**Businesses (lead audience):**

1. For Bengaluru café and darshini owners who give creators free meals and *"have no idea if anyone walks in"*, Mitra is the creator counter that shows how many customers each post brought, checked against your bill book, unlike Instagram likes or an agency's screenshots.
2. For barter-veteran owners in Jayanagar and Basavanagudi, Mitra turns every free meal you give a creator into a number: customers through the door, cost per customer.
3. For small businesses tired of creators asking for freebies, Mitra is where local creators come to you, and you only keep the ones who bring customers.

**Recommend 1.** Panel support: every business "buy" gave reason *need* (11 of 11), almost all in these words; "barter veteran" and "numbers-minded" buyers said yes 100% (thin groups). "Checked against your bill book" answers the top doubt (counts can't be trusted).

**Creators (second audience):**

1. For Bengaluru creators whose collab DMs are mostly free dosas and half sketchy, Mitra shows you the local businesses that fit you, and proves the customers you bring, so the next one pays cash, unlike apps where you apply and wait.
2. For Kannada creators who feel national brands skip them, Mitra is built for Bengaluru and Kannada, first collab free.
3. For growth-hungry creators, Mitra's Auto-DM turns "comment DOSA" into a counted customer for the café and reach for you.

**Recommend 1.** Panel support: the top creator objection was *"local cafés pay in food, not money"*; the most repeated flip was *"one real paid collab"*; counted customers are the lever that turns barter into cash. Version 3 is a supporting hook only: the board warned against positioning Mitra as a second Auto-DM app at Influish's price.

**One line for both sides (site, posters, bios):** *ಯಾವ ಕ್ರಿಯೇಟರ್ ಎಷ್ಟು ಗ್ರಾಹಕರನ್ನು ತಂದರು ಅಂತ ನೋಡಿ.* / *See which creator brought how many customers.*

## 4. Ten hooks

Each answers a panel objection or uses a buyer reason. Format noted.

1. **Leaflet headline (owner):** "You gave a creator a free lunch. How many customers came?" *(buy reason: need; "no idea if anyone walks in")*
2. **Walk-in opener (owner):** "We set it up at your counter in 10 minutes. You don't learn an app." *(objection: no time for another app)*
3. **Leaflet line (owner):** "Check every count against your bill book." *(objection: counts can't be trusted)*
4. **Owner-to-owner line:** "Ask the café down the road what their count was." *(flip: a nearby owner's real numbers)*
5. **Counter standee (customer):** "Came because of a reel? Show the coupon, get the offer." / ಕನ್ನಡ version *(objection: customers won't show a coupon)*
6. **WhatsApp opener (creator):** "10 Jayanagar places want creators this month. First collab free." *(objection: is it empty?)*
7. **WhatsApp line (creator):** "These ones pay cash, not dosa." (only real fee briefs) *(objection: cafés pay in food)*
8. **Site/hero line (creator):** "Free this month. First collab always free. We never take a cut." *(objections: ₹499 for what?; free-only creators)*
9. **3-second reel opener:** "This darshini now knows which creator brought 14 customers." (only after a real count) *(buy reason: proof)*
10. **Proof post headline:** "Week 1: X customers counted across Y shops. Here's what broke and what we fixed." *(objection: who are you / will you vanish; honesty builds trust)*

Plus one answer that every channel needs, because owners asked *"free, so how do you make money?"*: **"Creators who want more collabs pay ₹499 a year after their first free one. Businesses don't pay. We never take a cut of deals."**

## 5. Budget and numbers

**Budget split (no total was given; percentages of whatever you set aside for the first 30 days):**
- 60% walk-in rep time and travel (the only channel that converts the businesses who said "set it up for me")
- 20% printing (standees, leaflets) for live shops only
- 15% small thank-yous that aren't payments for reviews (e.g. covering a pilot shop's creator barter meal once) *(disclose any paid partnership; never pay for a review or testimonial)*
- 5% reserve
- 0% paid ads

**The most you can pay to win a customer.**
- A business pays nothing, so a business has no direct revenue; its value is the creators it brings in. Treat business acquisition as the cost of filling the room: cap it by the creator revenue it unlocks (below), not by its own revenue.
- A paying creator: ₹499 a year, minus payment fees (open question: about 2% with a gateway once built) ≈ **₹489 contribution in year one** (no delivery cost per creator beyond servers; the CFO should confirm).
- The panel's purchases per buyer (1.1) are free sign-ups, not payments. The share of sign-ups who pay after the free month is **unknown** (open question for the pilot). Worked example only: if 20% of creators who sign up pay, a creator sign-up is worth ₹489 × 20% ≈ **₹98**, so spend no more than about ₹98 to win a creator sign-up, and the cost of signing up a business must be covered by the creators it brings (e.g. a business that brings 5 creator sign-ups can cost up to ~₹490).
- Run `/founder-cfo` with the real rep cost to replace these with real numbers.

**Three numbers to watch weekly (and when to change something):**
1. **Counted share at the till:** redeemed coupons ÷ customers the owner believes came from the post (from the bill-book check). Below 50% at week 2 → simplify redemption (drop the PIN for a one-tap or staff-free flow) before adding shops.
2. **Businesses counting in neighbourhood A:** businesses with a redeemed customer in the last 14 days. Under 10 by L+14 → stop creator outreach, go back to walk-ins.
3. **Creators with a cash collab:** creators who got a fee (not barter) through Mitra. Zero by L+14 → don't enforce the Creator Pass; find and list fee-paying businesses first.

## Rules followed
- No fake reviews, counts, testimonials or "as seen on". Proof posts use real numbers, with the owner's permission, labelled as one shop's result.
- Panel quotes stay in this file; none go into ads.
- No earnings claims to creators ("earn ₹X"). Paid and gifted creator posts carry "Paid partnership" or #ad (ASCI). Not legal advice.
