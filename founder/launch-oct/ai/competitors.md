**Price range, monthly, for the closest item:** lowest ₹0 (Meta) / ₹199 (Manva), median about ₹1,999 among the AI tools, highest ₹12,000+ (agencies). ₹999 sits at the cheap end of the AI tools. ₹2,499 is a quarter of the price of an agency.

**Map (how much is done for the owner × price):** Meta is free and handles replies only. Manva, AIPoster and Interakt are cheap tools the owner still has to drive. ZocialOne and Synaptic cost ₹2k and do some of the work. Agencies charge ₹4k-12k and do all of it. Petpooja costs ₹20k+, does all of it including influencers, but is only for restaurants. The empty spot is "done for you, creators included, under ₹2,500".

### Complaints
1. **Wrong answers, or the AI goes silent.** One blog read 180 complaints about Meta's agent: it "goes quiet" (35), "states wrong facts or prices" (17), is not available to the business (31) ([Conciergr](https://conciergr.com/blog/meta-business-agent-problems); a competitor's blog, and they searched for complaints on purpose). Meta's own Help Center says: "AI-generated messages may not be accurate or appropriate."
2. **Billing traps after a trial.** Ocoya has 1.4 on Trustpilot from 224 reviews: "I canceled during the trial and they are still trying to charge me $1,908" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.ocoya.com)). Predis: "after subscription expired, they locked to all your files" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/predis.ai)). That second one is a single review, so the theme is thin.
3. **Support and refunds.** AiSensy has 3.4 from 58 reviews, and 45% of them are 1-star. Trustpilot's own summary says getting "a refund when something goes wrong can prove extraordinarily difficult" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/aisensy.com)). Interakt has 1.9 from 13 reviews (thin).
4. **Clinics are left out.** On Meta's platform version, "Health" is excluded from the agent (quoted by [Conciergr](https://conciergr.com/blog/meta-business-agent-problems) from Meta for Developers).

### Complaints
1. **Agencies hide the deal value and pay late.** One creator alleged she was told a campaign was worth "only ₹60,000" when the brand had paid more ([exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/influence-zone-news/how-creators-lose-money-without-knowing-it-156615.html)). Storyboard18 reports "withholding payments for months".
2. **Payouts cancelled, nobody answers.** On OPA: "Helpdesk tickets go unanswered, emails receive no response" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/opa-try-free-products/id1525779623)). Two such reviews were read, so the theme is thin.
3. **Paying for collabs that never come.** An Influish reviewer paid ₹499 and got no collab in six months (from the board brief, one review, thin).

## Rules

- **ASCI influencer guidelines:** every post with a "material connection" needs a label up front, and free food counts ([ASCI PDF](https://www.ascionline.in/social/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ASCI-Influencer-Guidelines.pdf)). Barter campaigns run by Idea 1 must carry #ad or "Collaboration".
- **ASCI rules on AI-made content** (published 17-29 Sep 2026, in force three months later): a label is **mandatory** for "synthetically generated influencers and ambassadors". It is **not required** for "generating advertising copy" or translations. Fabricated testimonials are **prohibited** ([guidelines](https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Guidelines-for-Responsible-Labelling-of-Synthetically-Generated-Content-in-Advertising.pdf), [press release](https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Press-Release_Guidelines-for-Responsible-Labelling-of-Synthetically-Generated-Content-in-Advertising.pdf)). So AI-written captions are fine. AI-made "customer reviews" or an AI persona posing as a creator are not.
- **Meta publishing:** Mitra needs `instagram_business_content_publish` plus `instagram_business_basic` and App Review. The account must be professional. The limit is 100 API posts per 24 hours, though one Meta reference page says 50 ([Postman/Meta](https://www.postman.com/meta/instagram/folder/23987686-bc459e67-42aa-4ea0-ad25-e5a6e42c3a83), [Blotato](https://www.blotato.com/blog/instagram-posting-api)). Comment-to-DM allows one DM per comment within 7 days; normal DMs have a 24-hour reply window ([FRIDAI](https://fridai.fansnetwork.ai/en/blog/instagram-auto-reply-tools)).
- **WhatsApp costs:** from 1 Oct 2026, marketing messages cost ₹1.09 each and service messages cost ₹0.145 after 1,000 free a month ([AiSensy](https://aisensy.com/pricing)). The weekly report and the approval pings each cost money.

None of these rules blocks either idea.

## The gap

**Idea 1: the ₹999 tier has no gap.** Meta does the replies and leads for free in Indian languages. Manva already does Kannada captions, posting from WhatsApp and owner approval for ₹999. The gap is only in the **₹2,499 tier**: creator campaigns plus customers counted at the counter, done for the owner over WhatsApp. Petpooja is the only one bundling local influencers, and only for restaurants on its own POS at ₹20k+. Agencies charge ₹4k-12k and leave footfall uncounted. That is Mitra's marketplace and customer counter with AI on top: sell that, not a caption bot.

**Idea 2: the gap is small.** Socialji gives the same four features for free, and Richi already works over WhatsApp. What nobody does well yet: a manager in ₹ for Bengaluru creators with 2K-100K followers, where offers come by DM or WhatsApp in Kannada or English, with checks for India's registration-fee scams and rates in ₹ for barter vs cash. A fee on closed deals is hard to collect when deals close off-platform. The ₹199/yr price matches what the panel called a "bargain".

## Who could copy fastest

- **Idea 1:** Meta (agent plus Instagram, already free). Petpooja (has influencers, 1.5 lakh restaurants). Manva (could add a creator marketplace).
- **Idea 2:** Influish. It already has the rate calculator, invoices, AI captions and lakhs of creators at ₹499/yr, and could add AI drafting in weeks. Wishlink is next (100,000+ creators, Meta partner).
