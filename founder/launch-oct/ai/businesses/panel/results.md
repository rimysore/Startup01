**11 buy · 9 pass** (55% buy) out of 20 simulated buyers. Seed 571712, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

| reason | buyers | in their words |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| habit | 3 | "I already pay an agency to run Instagram for my outlets, and my marketing person handles them. I'm not going to switch to an AI beta from a small team and approve posts on WhatsApp between orders at the counter." (P001) · "My regulars and Google reviews already keep the chairs full, and when I have an offer I just drop it in the local WhatsApp group. I've never needed Instagram or creators, so I don't see why I'd start paying for it now, even with a free month." (P010) |
| need | 3 | "I already pay a marketing person to plan our posts, so an AI sending me three captions a week doesn't take anything off my plate. And the creator-collab part is exactly what burned me before: likes, no customers." (P007) · "My regulars and Google reviews fill the place already, and I am standing at the counter from morning to night, so I will not be shooting reels or approving posts on WhatsApp every Monday. | trust | 3 | "I already paid a creator once and got likes, no customers, and this talks about creators again. ## What would flip a no

- Proof from another Bengaluru chain my size that the comment-to-WhatsApp enquiries actually brought in more bookings than our agency gets, so I could hand it to my marketing person as an add-on, not a replacement.
- Proof from another Bengaluru outlet like mine that the DM-to-WhatsApp enquiries turned into actual orders or bookings, with numbers, not just reach.
- Seeing a café or darshini near me in South Bengaluru that has used it for a few months and can show me real enquiries in their WhatsApp that turned into customers.
- Another salon or gym owner I know in my area showing me real new bookings that came from it in the first month, not just views and followers.
- If another darshini or café near me showed me their UPI numbers going up after using it, I would try the free month.
- Seeing it working for a salon or gym owner I know in my area, and meeting someone from the team in person who sets it up for me.
- Real numbers from two or three salons or gyms in South Bengaluru showing how many bookings came in through WhatsApp each month, and the names of the creators they worked with.
- Another café owner nearby that I know telling me it actually brought them new customers, and seeing it work on their page first.
- Another darshini owner near me telling me it actually brought new customers, and someone from the team setting up the Instagram connection on my phone in person.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
