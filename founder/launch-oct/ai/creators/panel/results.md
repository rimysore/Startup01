## What would flip a no

- Two or three creators I actually know from my WhatsApp groups vouching that it caught a real scam or got them paid on a late deal, and a way to use the scam check by just pasting an offer without connecting my account.
- If it actually sent me paid offers from Bengaluru businesses looking for Kannada creators, I'd pay the ₹199 without thinking.
- Proof that the auto-DM uses Instagram's official API and stays inside its limits, like a few Bengaluru creators my size using it for months with no flags or reach drop.
- If it actually brought me collab leads or helped me grow followers, for example by matching me with Bengaluru brands, instead of only managing deals I find myself.
- If the free version caught a real scam or got me a noticeably higher rate on a real deal, I'd think about the 5% option. I still wouldn't pay up front.
- Proof from other Bengaluru creators my size that the Pro auto-DM to commenters actually grew their followers and reach, without any risk to their account.
- Seeing a real registered company with named founders and a support contact, plus a creator I know in Bengaluru saying it actually got them paid on a late collab.
- If it actually turned one of my barter offers into a paid deal, and I could see the real company behind it with a support number in Bengaluru, I'd pay the ₹199.
- If Mitra actually brought me paid brand offers, or proof that its counter-offers turned barter deals into paid ones for nano creators like me in Bengaluru.
- If people in my creator WhatsApp groups said Mitra got them a paid deal or got a late payment out of a brand, and the site showed a real registered company with a named founder and a support contact.
- If a creator I know from my WhatsApp groups said they'd used it for a few months and it actually got them paid on a late deal, and I could use the scam check by just pasting an offer without connecting my Instagram at all.
- If its counter-offer actually turned one of my barter DMs into a paid deal, I'd happily pay the 5% or ₹199.
