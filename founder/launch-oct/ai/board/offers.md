# Offers lens
Score: 6/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## Verdict in one paragraph
The market is middling: some owners pay (agencies charge ₹3,999–12,000), but 3 of 20 say word of mouth is enough, and growth is not shown. On the value equation, effort is strong (WhatsApp approval is the top yes reason, 8 of 11) and time is one month. Likelihood is weak: no proof, a beta asking for Instagram access, owners burned by creators. ₹999 posting alone is a commodity (Manva charges the same; Meta's replies are free). The pay-on-results creator campaign is what could make saying no feel foolish. Fund it if the offer is rebuilt around that campaign with a defined guarantee.

## What to charge for, and the prices
- **Headline offer: "Bengaluru creator campaign, run for you, pay only if enquiries come."** Mitra's fee is ₹999 per campaign, charged only if the report shows at least N enquiries the owner confirms. N is an open question; set it before launch so "shows its enquiries" is not vague.
- **AI Marketing Manager: ₹999/month as the included base,** first month free. It sits between the panel's bargain (₹699) and expensive (₹1,750) medians. Test ₹1,499 only once cases exist.
- **Guarantee:** if month 2 brings no confirmed enquiry, month 3 is free. The CFO must cost this first.
- **Real scarcity:** founding pricing for the first 20 businesses in one neighbourhood. A real cap: campaigns are run by hand.
- **Creators: free.** Mitra charges creators nothing and takes no cut.

## What to cut
- All paid creator tiers (₹199/yr, 5% of deals). 0 of 20 would pay.
- The ₹2,499 bundle. Most of the panel refused it.
- The old YouTube branch, unless it improves Kannada captions.
- Posting as the headline. Keep it inside the plan.

## What would kill it (top 3)
1. **Trials don't convert.** All 11 yeses are free-month trials, and most say they will cancel without enquiries that become orders. The CFO's +₹72k rests on an unknown conversion rate.
2. **First campaigns flop.** If early creators bring nothing, the pay-on-results fee earns ₹0 and confirms the "creators burned me" fear.
3. **It reads as a caption bot.** If campaigns lag, Mitra is a Manva clone against free Meta; an App Review delay worsens it.

## First 20 paying businesses and 200 creators, no shop visits
- **Creators first, through the free paste-to-check scam tool:** Instagram DMs and Bengaluru creator WhatsApp groups. Promise paid campaigns only once businesses sign.
- **Businesses via barter history:** ask creators which cafés gave them free meals; DM businesses with recent collab posts. Barter veterans said yes most.
- **Remote setup:** a 10-minute video call and the WhatsApp bot.
- **One neighbourhood:** the first 3–5 confirmed results become the "nearby owner showed results" proof that flips a no.
- **Open:** DM reply and creator-to-café referral rates are unmeasured.

## What to build first
1. WhatsApp-link enquiry tracking and the Sunday report with tap-to-confirm. This is the proof that moves likelihood.
2. The weekly plan and the WhatsApp approval flow.
3. The campaign flow on top of the matching, invites and deal room already built.
4. Submit Meta App Review in week 1, in parallel. Publishing and the scheduler follow; until then the owner posts approved content.
5. Measure the real AI cost per business for a week.

## Defence against Meta, Manva, Influish
- **Meta:** don't sell replies. Sell creators plus confirmed enquiries.
- **Manva:** its public pages show no creator marketplace. Confirmed enquiries per creator, per neighbourhood, is the edge; it is copyable, so density and speed matter.
- **Influish:** it already serves creators at 0%; Mitra wins by bringing local paid campaigns, not better tools.
- **Overall:** the offer and local data defend, not the software.

## Conditions
- Define the campaign-fee trigger (N confirmed enquiries) and a guarantee the CFO can afford.
- Visible company, named founders and a support number before the first charge.
- Native-speaker review of early Kannada captions.
- Measure trial-to-paid, confirmed enquiries per campaign and AI cost; re-run the CFO with campaign fees.
- Submit Meta App Review now.
- One neighbourhood until 20 are paying.
