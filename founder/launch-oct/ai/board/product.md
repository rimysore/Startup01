# Product lens
Score: 6/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## Verdict in one paragraph
The clearest Mitra yet: the coupon seam is gone and the pitch fits one sentence. But it is still two products: ₹999 AI posting, which Manva and Meta already sell, and done-for-you creator campaigns, which nobody sells under ₹20k. The one thing is the campaign: a local creator posts, enquiries land in the owner's WhatsApp, and the owner taps to confirm which ones became orders. Everything else feeds that moment or waits. Up from 5 for fewer seams; not higher because the proof parts (enquiry tracking, Sunday report, WhatsApp campaign flow) are unbuilt and all evidence is simulated.

## What to charge for, and the prices
- **₹999/month, first month free.** Panel range (bargain ₹699, expensive ₹1,750) supports it; hold it until real conversions.
- **The free month includes one creator campaign run by Mitra.** Trial conversion depends on month-1 proof, and a creator post is its likeliest source. The business pays the creator; barter is fine.
- **Further campaigns ₹999 each,** charged only after the post is live and the report shows its enquiries. Define "shows enquiries" in one line the owner can read (open question: the threshold).
- **Creators: free.** No Pro tier this year.
- Three money flows is a lot: show them on one WhatsApp card per campaign.

## What to cut
- **Auto-publishing to the business's Instagram and the scheduler, for now.** Send the weekly plan as ready-to-post WhatsApp drafts that the owner posts in one tap. That removes a Meta permission, a build and the "handing Instagram to a beta startup" pass.
- **AI posting as a headline.** Include it; never sell it.
- **The old YouTube branch** for this launch.
- **The ₹2,499 bundle and any creator fee.**

## What would kill it (top 3)
1. **Month 1 proves nothing.** Few enquiries or ignored tap-to-confirm means trials cancel; the CFO model rests on this.
2. **Seams with no one on the ground.** Late or missing creator posts, a wrong brief, generic Kannada, and a founder in the USA. One bad campaign becomes the story owners share.
3. **Meta dependency.** App Review delays, an Auto-DM flag, or Meta's free agent taking over enquiry handling inside WhatsApp.

## First 20 paying businesses and 200 creators, no shop visits
- **Creators first, through the free paste-to-check scam tool.** Share it in Bengaluru creator WhatsApp groups and Instagram DMs, one neighbourhood first.
- **Creators bring their barter cafés.** Each creator forwards a Mitra WhatsApp link to businesses they already post for: "your next collab, run for you, results on WhatsApp." The earlier 1-in-10 activation guess is unmeasured.
- **Target the yes segment:** barter veterans and owners burned by influencers.
- **10-minute video call setup** with each owner, from a named Mitra number.
- **First results are the sales material:** a nearby owner's confirmed orders was the panel's main flip.

## What to build first
1. A trust page: registered name, named founders, support number, "nothing posts or sends without your OK".
2. WhatsApp-link enquiry tracking and the Sunday report with tap-to-confirm.
3. The campaign flow over WhatsApp, on the built matching, invites and deal room.
4. Weekly plan as WhatsApp drafts, with a native-speaker check of the Kannada.
5. Meta App Review for what is built. Publishing comes later (open question: one submission or two).

## Defence against Meta, Manva, Influish
- **Meta:** don't compete on replies; own the creator side and the confirmed-order record.
- **Manva:** sells software the owner drives; Mitra sells a result. Win one neighbourhood's creators before Manva adds a marketplace.
- **Influish:** it has creators, but a public review says collabs never came. Promise creators a paid local campaign; track and publish days to first paid campaign.

## Conditions
- One neighbourhood until 20 businesses pay.
- Measure: free-to-paid conversion, enquiries per campaign, tap-to-confirm rate, creator on-time post rate, real AI and WhatsApp cost per business.
- Open questions: share of cash vs barter campaigns (creators' flip needs cash), the billing threshold, who fixes a failed campaign.
- The founder decides before any code changes, and the founder submits Meta App Review.
