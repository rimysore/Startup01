## Constraints
- The founder is in the USA. There are no shop visits in Bengaluru and no field team.
- The founder dropped the coupon counter: it is too messy and doesn't fit every business.
- Meta App Review has not been submitted. The founder will do it.

## The revised model
- **Pitch to businesses** (cafés, salons, gyms, boutiques, small chains in Bengaluru): "Bengaluru creators for your business, run for you, results on WhatsApp."
- **AI Marketing Manager: ₹999/month, first month free.** Owners approve on WhatsApp; nothing is posted without their OK.
  - Weekly post and reel plan, with captions in Kannada and English.
  - Comment-to-DM: someone who comments "price" or "menu" gets a DM with the business's WhatsApp link, so enquiries land in the owner's chat.
  - Sunday WhatsApp report: views, enquiries, and "which of these became orders?" with tap-to-confirm.
- **Creator campaign: about ₹999 per campaign**, charged only after the creator's post is live and the report shows its enquiries.
  - Mitra's AI picks matching creators, writes the brief and runs the collab.
  - The business pays the creator. Mitra takes no cut from creators.
- **Creators: free AI talent tools.** Scam check by pasting an offer (no Instagram connection needed), fair rate, media kit, and payment reminders that go out only after the creator approves them. Creators find paid campaigns from Mitra businesses through these tools.
- **Already built** (dev): Instagram Login with insights and audience, Auto-DM with webhooks, creator matching, invites, deal room, inbox scam checks, fair-rate range, media kit, WhatsApp bot.
- **Old YouTube-product branch:** AI images, trends and Kannada slang.
- **Still to build:** publishing to the business's Instagram (needs the `instagram_business_content_publish` permission and Meta App Review), a scheduler, WhatsApp-link enquiry tracking, and the weekly plan and approval flow.

## Evidence (simulated panels, same buyer cards as the coupon panels)
- **Businesses: 11 of 20 would try the ₹999 plan** (the coupon pitch got 10 of 20). All 11 are free-month trials, and most say they will cancel unless enquiries turn into real orders.
  - Yes reasons: convenience 8 ("just reply on WhatsApp, no coupons, no staff steps").
  - Pass reasons: habit 3 (an agency or a nephew already does it), need 3 (word of mouth is enough), trust 3 (handing Instagram to a beta startup).
  - Most refused a ₹2,499 bundle with a creator campaign; creators had burned them before.
  - Price medians (₹/month): too cheap 299, bargain 699, expensive 1,750, too expensive 3,250.
- **Creators: 0 of 20 would pay for the talent manager** (₹199/yr or 5% of deals). Pass reasons: trust 10, need 8 (barter only).
  - **12 of 20 say the flip is "Mitra brings me paid collabs"**, which is what the business side creates.
  - Price medians (₹/yr): too cheap 0, bargain 99.
- **CFO** (₹999/month only, no campaign fees, no rep; all costs except Razorpay are estimates; AI cost about ₹150 per business per month):
  - Contribution is ₹795 per business-month; fixed costs are ₹10,167/month; break-even is about 13 paying businesses.
  - Assumed ramp: 3–8 new paying businesses a month, 10% churn, about 43 paying by month 12.
  - Year 1: **+₹72,469**, about ₹40k cash needed, profitable from month 4. 20% fewer businesses gives +₹33.6k; half as many gives about −₹24.8k.
- **Competitors:**
  - Manva.ai: ₹199–1,999/mo, Kannada from ₹999, WhatsApp approvals.
  - ZocialOne: ₹1,999/mo.
  - Meta Business Agent on WhatsApp: free replies and leads in Indian languages, still rolling out.
  - Bengaluru agencies: ₹3,999–12,000/mo.
  - Petpooja: bundles influencers at ₹20–40k, restaurants only.
  - Socialji: free AI talent manager.
  - Influish: 0% commission.
  - Gap: done-for-you creator campaigns with checkable results over WhatsApp, under ₹20k. The ₹999 AI posting part on its own has no gap.

## The question for the board
Should Mitra launch this revised model? Score it, and say what to change:
- what to charge for, and at what price;
- what to cut;
- how the first 20 paying businesses and 200 creators arrive without shop visits;
- what to build first, since the founder must also finish Meta setup;
- how to defend against Meta, Manva and Influish.
