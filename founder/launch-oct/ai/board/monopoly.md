# Monopoly lens
Score: 6/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## Verdict in one paragraph
₹999 AI posting is a crowded market and cannot win. Manva sells Kannada captions with WhatsApp approval at ₹999, and Meta's agent handles replies for free. What can win is done-for-you creator campaigns with results the owner can check, which nobody offers under ₹20k. That is a small market Mitra could own (barter-giving cafés and salons in one neighbourhood). It is two-sided, so it could build a local network effect and a record nobody else has: which creator brought which confirmed orders. The model beats the coupon model (no staff steps, CFO break-even at 13 businesses), but it is not a monopoly yet. It has zero users, the panels are simulated, and campaign fees are unmodelled.

## What to charge for, and the prices
- **Creator campaign, the part with no competitor:** ₹999, paid only after the post is live and the report shows its enquiries. Agencies charge ₹4–12k and Petpooja ₹20–40k, so test ₹1,499 once creators have records.
- **AI Marketing Manager:** ₹999/month, first month free, sold as the base that comes with campaigns. Never cut the price to fight Manva or Meta.
- **Creators: free.** No pass and no cut. They are the supply side.

## What to cut
- The creator pass and the 5% deal fee (0 of 20 in the panel).
- The ₹2,499 bundle (refused).
- The old YouTube branch (AI images, trends, slang).
- Launching city-wide or in every category.

## What would kill it (top 3)
1. **Trials don't convert.** If owners never tap "which became orders", there is no proof, the free months churn and the data record never forms.
2. **The network stays barter-only.** Creators' flip was paid collabs (12 of 20). Without them, the free tools lose to Socialji and Influish.
3. **A neighbour with distribution copies it.** Manva could add creators, Petpooja could widen beyond restaurants, or Influish or Wishlink could add local businesses.

## First 20 paying businesses and 200 creators, no shop visits
- **Creators first, in one neighbourhood.** DM creators who already post barter collabs tagged there, and share the free paste-to-check scam tool in Bengaluru creator WhatsApp groups. The pitch: "paid campaigns from businesses near you, no cut."
- **Creators bring businesses.** Each creator invites the cafés and salons they already do barter with.
- **Direct DM to businesses whose posts tag local creators,** since they are proven barter-givers, the panel's best yes. Set each one up on a 10-minute video call.
- **Proof travels.** Share one Sunday report with confirmed orders (with permission) to same-type businesses nearby.
- **Open questions:** the creator-to-business activation rate, cost per paying business, and campaigns per business. None is known yet.

## What to build first
1. **Submit Meta App Review now,** with every permission in one request.
2. **Tracked WhatsApp enquiry link per creator post, plus the Sunday tap-to-confirm report.** Neither needs publishing, and together they make the proof and the record.
3. **The campaign flow,** built on the matching and deal room that already exist.
4. **Weekly plan and approval last.** Until Meta approves publishing, owners post the approved drafts themselves.

## Defence against Meta, Manva, Influish
- **Meta:** it answers chats and runs no local creator campaigns. Use it as a channel, not a rival.
- **Manva:** a tool with no creator side. The defence is density: most of the active creators in one neighbourhood, plus their confirmed-orders history.
- **Influish:** pan-India, creators pay, no guaranteed collabs. Mitra is local, free for creators, paid by businesses, and shows results.
- **Honest limit:** a local network can be copied city by city. It lasts only if Mitra reaches density first.

## Conditions
- One neighbourhood and two categories until 13 businesses pay.
- Every pitch leads with campaigns, with AI posting included.
- Track weekly: trial-to-paid rate, confirmed orders per campaign, the share of paid campaigns and cost per paying business.
- Model campaign fees in the CFO before changing prices.
- Make the company visible: registered name, named founders, a support number.
- The founder submits App Review and makes all code decisions.
