# Board: the revised model, AI-run creator campaigns (October 2026)

The brief is in `board/brief.md`. The three memos are `board/offers.md`, `board/monopoly.md` and `board/product.md`. The evidence comes from simulated panels, so real buyers still need to confirm it.

**Verdict: 3 of 3 FUND IF, at 6/10 each. This is the highest score so far.** Earlier boards scored 4/10 (October), 2/10 (cash referrals) and 5/10 (coupon model).

## What all three agree on
- **Lead with the creator campaign.** It is the part nobody offers under ₹20k. The ₹999/month AI manager is the base that comes with it. Don't cut that price to fight Manva or Meta.
- **Pay on results.** ₹999 per campaign, charged only after a set number of enquiries the owner has confirmed. The exact number is still open and must be set before launch.
- **Creators stay free.** Cut every paid creator tier, the 5% fee, the ₹2,499 bundle and the old YouTube branch.
- **One neighbourhood and two categories.** Start with businesses that already give creators free meals, until there are 13 paying businesses (the CFO's break-even).
- **The top risk:** free-month trials don't convert because month 1 shows no proof, or the owner never confirms orders.

## What they add
- **Product:** include one creator campaign in the free month so proof comes early. Send the weekly plan as WhatsApp drafts the owner posts in one tap. That removes the publishing permission, a build, and the "handing my Instagram to a startup" objection.
- **Offers:** a guarantee. If month 2 brings no confirmed enquiry, month 3 is free (the CFO should cost this first). Founding pricing is capped at 20 businesses in one neighbourhood; that limit is real, so it is honest scarcity.
- **Monopoly:** the moat is the local two-sided network, plus the record of which creator brought which confirmed orders. The fastest copiers are Manva, Petpooja, Influish and Wishlink, because they already have the customers.

## Build order (merged; the founder decides)
1. **Week 1: submit Meta App Review** for what's built: Instagram Login and insights, plus comments and messages for Auto-DM. Decide whether publishing waits for a second submission. Product says it can wait if posts go out as drafts.
2. **A trust page:** registered company, named founders, a support number, and a statement that nothing is ever sent without approval.
3. **WhatsApp-link enquiry tracking and the Sunday report** with tap-to-confirm.
4. **The campaign flow** over WhatsApp: match, brief, post goes live, results, then the bill.
5. **The weekly plan as drafts,** with a native speaker checking the Kannada.

## First users, no shop visits
- Seed creators first in one neighbourhood, through Instagram DMs and creator WhatsApp groups.
- Creators bring the businesses they already collab with.
- Owners are set up over WhatsApp video.
- Each confirmed result becomes proof for the next owner nearby.

**Unmeasured:** activation rate, trial-to-paid rate, cost per paying business, campaigns per business, and the share of campaigns that pay cash rather than barter.

## Conditions before money or code
- Set the "results" threshold and the guarantee, and model campaign fees in the CFO.
- Measure the real AI and WhatsApp cost per business.
- The founder keeps App Review and every code and production decision.
