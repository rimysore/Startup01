# Board: Mitra (beta), October 2026

**Vote: 3 fund if, 0 pass. Average score 4.0 / 10.** Memos: `board/offers.md`, `board/monopoly.md`, `board/product.md`. Brief: `board/brief.md`.

## The risks more than one member raised (most dangerous first)

1. **Counts that read low (all three).** The whole difference rests on a staff member typing a PIN in the rush and a customer showing a coupon. If the counter undercounts, the one thing that sets Mitra apart becomes proof that creators don't work. Nobody knows the real redemption rate yet.
2. **An empty marketplace (all three).** Zero businesses and zero creators, no written plan for how the first ones arrive or what each costs, and creators' top doubt is "is it empty?". A creator's first visit may show little, and mostly barter.
3. **Charging the wrong side (Offers, Monopoly).** The side with the pain and the money (businesses) pays nothing; the side that pays (creators) has the least money, the most trust doubts and a same-price alternative in Influish. The creator ₹499 asks for payment before there is proof of a cash collab, which is the exact complaint in Influish's public reviews.
4. **The creator offer copies Influish (Monopoly, Product).** Auto-DM, 0% commission and ₹499 a year are Influish's offer. On the creator side Mitra has no clear thing of its own yet; the counter is the difference.
5. **Trust and dependency gaps (all three).** No Meta app (so no Verified numbers, no Auto-DM for real users), no registered company, no escrow, no named person to report a non-paying business to.

## Where they disagree

- **Auto-DM.** The Offers lens counts it as part of a strong value stack (comments turn into counted customers). The Product lens would put it behind the counter and launch without it. The Monopoly lens says lead with the counter and don't position Mitra as "a second Auto-DM app".
- **Who pays.** The Offers lens wants a business price tested once counts are proven (per customer, or for the counter). The Monopoly lens questions charging creators at all. The Product lens leaves pricing aside and asks to cut the paid pass until the counter works.
- **How much to cut.** The Product lens would strip the launch to the counter plus the matching that feeds it. The other two keep the marketplace but want supply in place before any creator is asked to pay.

## Conditions (checklist for the rest of the pack)

- [ ] **Pick the one thing:** position Mitra as "count the customers a creator brings" (counter first, matching feeds it). *(Product, Monopoly; marketing and brand)*
- [ ] **Prove the counter at the till:** someone from Mitra sets up a few outlets in person; a pilot matches redeemed coupons to the bill book; publish the counted share as the pilot's main number. *(all three; ops, launch)*
- [ ] **Make redeeming effortless in a rush** (fewer steps than a 4-digit PIN; WhatsApp-first for tech-shy owners), tested at real outlets before the wider launch. *(Product, panel; ops)*
- [ ] **Own one neighbourhood first:** a named list of target businesses and a target share counting customers weekly. *(Monopoly; launch, ops)*
- [ ] **Supply before the paywall:** a set number of businesses live, including some cash briefs, before any creator is asked for ₹499; no creator's first visit is an empty room. *(all three; launch)*
- [ ] **Write the first-100 plan:** who on the ground signs people up, cost per business and per creator. *(Monopoly; marketing, CFO)*
- [ ] **A guarantee the founder can afford** for the Creator Pass (e.g. no paid collab within a stated period → refund or extension). *(Offers; offer, CFO)*
- [ ] **Test a business price** once counts are proven (per counted customer or for the counter). *(Offers; pricing, CFO)*
- [ ] **Trust basics before charging anyone:** register the company, a named reachable operator, a way to report a non-paying business, at least one cash collab in the free month that a creator will vouch for. *(all three; ops)*
- [ ] **Remove the Meta single point of failure:** submit App Review now; make sure the counter and collabs work without it; don't show "Verified" until it passes. *(Monopoly, Product, Offers; ops)*
- [ ] **Answer "Free, so how do you make money?"** in public copy, so owners don't read free as "will vanish or charge later". *(panel; marketing)*

## The strongest version the board can see

Not quite what was pitched. The strongest Mitra is **the way a Bengaluru café knows which creator brought customers in**: a customer counter that owners trust, set up in person in one neighbourhood, with redemption simple enough to survive the 1 pm rush and checked against the bill book. The creator marketplace is how those owners find creators, and the counted results become the creator's proof that they deserve cash, not a free dosa. Auto-DM is useful as the bridge (comment → coupon → counted customer) but not the headline, because on its own it is Influish's product at Influish's price. Money comes later from the side with the pain and the budget, once the counts are proven; the creator pass only makes sense once a creator can see paid collabs waiting for them.
