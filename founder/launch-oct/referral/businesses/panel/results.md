# Buyer panel: Mitra (beta), creators and a customer counter for Bengaluru local businesses

**11 buy · 9 pass** (55% buy) out of 20 simulated buyers. Seed 662944, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## Why they pass

| reason | buyers | in their words |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| trust | 5 | "I already pay an agency to handle Instagram and creators, and I'm on the counter from 7 to 2, so I don't have time to train staff on PINs and coupons for a beta app. Free plus paying me ₹200 a month sounds like it'll change the moment I rely on it." (P003) · "I give free meals to creators all the time and never know if anyone comes, so the coupon counter is the one part that interests me. But a beta startup that says it will pay me ₹200 a month for nothing sounds too good, and I'm not handing my Google login and my staff's time to people who could vanish next month." (P010) |
| convenience | 3 | "I'm at the counter from 7 to 2 and I don't want another app, another login, and my staff fumbling with PINs while customers wait. Creators already message me on WhatsApp, so I don't see why I need this." (P005) · "I run the counter myself during the rush, I am not going to stop and punch PINs for coupons, and I don't sign up for new apps. If my nephew wants creators he can message them on WhatsApp like he does now." (P006) |
| habit | 1 | "I already pay my agency to handle Instagram, and my counter boy has no time to type PINs for coupons during the morning rush. Rs 200 a month doesn't cover the hassle, and I'd rather not start leaning on a beta app run by a small team." (P004) |

## What would flip a no

- My agency running it for me, plus a written commitment on what it will cost after the pilot, and proof from a salon like mine that the coupons brought in real paying customers.
- My agency running it for me, or a café owner I know showing me real walk-ins and cost per customer from it, plus it being written down that the business side stays free.
- If it worked fully on WhatsApp with no new app or sign-in, and a salon owner I know showed me it actually brought paying customers.
- If it worked over WhatsApp with no login, and someone I know in the area showed me real extra customers it brought them.
- If it worked fully over WhatsApp, no app or Google login, and the coupon check was just a UPI-style scan or a WhatsApp message my staff already know how to do.
- Another café or darshini owner I know in my area showing me that real customers walked in with Mitra coupons and the ₹200 actually got paid.
- Seeing a salon or gym near me that I know get real, repeat customers from good local creators through it, with the coupon numbers to show for it.
- Another café or darshini owner I know nearby telling me they got real new customers through it, with the coupon numbers to prove it.
- Another café owner I know nearby telling me it actually brought them new paying customers, or my nephew setting it up and handling it for me.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
