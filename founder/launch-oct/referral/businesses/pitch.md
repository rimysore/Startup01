Mitra (beta): local creators who fit your business, a customer counter, and a way to earn. Bengaluru, Kannada and English.
Add your business free (Google sign-in, 2 minutes). Creators near you see it; you see who is interested, with their verified Instagram followers and average views. Every creator post gets a coupon your staff redeem with a PIN, so you see customers and cost per customer for each creator.
Bring creators: share your Mitra link with creators who come to you for collabs. When a creator you brought buys the ₹499-a-year Creator Pass, you get ₹150. Creators who join get their first collab free.
Stay active (list an offer and redeem coupons each month) and Mitra pays you ₹200 a month during the pilot. Creators on the pass make short promotional videos for participating businesses.
Free for businesses. Beta: a small team, Bengaluru only for now.
