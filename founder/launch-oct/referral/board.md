# Board: the referral model for the market side (October 2026)

**Vote: 3 pass. Average score 2.0 / 10.** Memos: `board/offers.md`, `board/monopoly.md`, `board/product.md`. Brief: `board/brief.md`.

## The risks all three raised

1. **It looks like the registration-fee scam.** A café invites a creator, and the café earns ₹150 when the creator pays ₹499. Public Indian warnings define "pay to get a collab" as the classic creator scam, and Mitra's own offer checker flags it. Creator buyers fell from 6/20 to **0/20** with this pitch.
2. **It buys nothing on the business side.** Businesses signing up stayed at 11/20, the same as without it. Buyers said the ₹150 and ₹200 "don't move" them, and trust-based passes rose from 3 to 5 ("free *and* paying me sounds like a catch").
3. **It copies Influish, at a worse rate.** Influish already pays ₹250 (50%) per ₹499 pass referred. Matching it gives Mitra no edge; beating it eats the pass.
4. **It needs things Mitra doesn't have:** a registered company, PAN of each payee, TDS on commissions, GST advice, ASCI disclosure of the material connection, and a payout system (payments are still manual UPI).

**Also from the creator panel:** "pass holders make promo videos for businesses" reads as unpaid work that undercuts creators' rates, even from creators for whom ₹499 is nothing.

## Where they disagree

Only on what to offer instead. The Offers lens would give businesses a results guarantee (a free second creator if the first post brings fewer than an agreed number of counted customers in 30 days) and reward creators who bring creators with free months, not cash. The Monopoly lens would reward businesses with visibility earned on the counter (a featured slot, a "customers counted" badge). The Product lens would add no incentive at launch at all; if one comes later, tie it to redeemed customers.

## What would change the vote

- [ ] No money flows from a creator's payment to the business that invited them.
- [ ] Promotional videos are optional and paid at the creator's rate, never a condition of the pass.
- [ ] Any business reward is tied to customers counted at the counter (or given in kind: setup, visibility), not to creators' payments.
- [ ] If referral rewards exist at all, creators refer creators and get free pass months; disclosed under ASCI.
- [ ] Payouts of any kind wait for a registered company and proper tax handling.

## The strongest version the board sees

Keep the founder's instinct (use businesses to bring creators) and drop the money. **A café invites the creators it already knows to do their free first collab through Mitra, and the reward for both sides is the result: customers counted at the counter.** The invite reads as a café sharing an opportunity, not selling a membership. Mitra pays for growth in setup visits and, later, a results guarantee to businesses, not in commissions. The asset that grows is the verified record of which creators bring real walk-ins to which counters, which Influish can't copy by changing a referral rate.
