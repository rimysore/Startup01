# Board brief: a referral model for Mitra's market side (October 2026)

Read the background first: /home/user/Startup01/founder/launch-oct/board/brief.md (what Mitra is, what is built, status, the competitor Influish). The previous board voted 3 "fund if" at 4/10 and asked Mitra to lead with the customer counter, fill the marketplace with businesses before asking creators to pay, and fix trust basics.

## The new idea (from the founder)
To bring brands, local businesses and others onto the market side:
1. When a creator they brought to Mitra pays the ₹499-a-year Creator Pass, the business gets a share of that payment (tested at ₹150, i.e. ~30%).
2. Participating businesses get promotional videos made by creators on the pass.
3. Mitra also pays participating businesses a small amount for staying active (tested at ₹200 a month during the pilot).
Creators' first collab stays free; businesses still pay nothing.

## Evidence gathered today (all in /home/user/Startup01/founder/launch-oct/referral/)
**Competitors (competitors.md):** Influish already runs "Refer & Earn": ₹250 to anyone whose code is used for a ₹499 Collab Pass (50%). Other creator platforms pay referrers 20–40% of subscriptions (Lusfera 40%, Createntor 30%, Subscriby 5–30%, Favikon 20% of brand subscriptions). Safer programs pay from the business side or from results (Creator Guard: "the creator never pays… asking a creator for a fee… gets you excluded"; FlixSell: % of creators' earnings). Public Indian scam warnings define "pay a registration/access fee to get a brand collab" as the classic creator scam ("Money flows from the brand to the creator"). ASCI treats any benefit as a material connection requiring disclosure. Commission payouts need TDS (2%, threshold ₹15,000–20,000/yr), PAN of payees, likely GST advice, and a registered company.

**Simulated buyer panels with this pitch (20 each; simulated, buy rates are an upper bound):**
- Creators: **0 buy · 20 pass** (was 6/20 without the referral model, same customer profiles). Reasons: trust 11, values 3, habit 3, need 2, price 1. Repeated: "the café gets ₹150 when I buy, so the invite feels like a sales pitch / I'm the one being sold"; "pass holders make promo videos for businesses = paying to do unpaid work, cheapens my rates"; burned-before creators compare it to registration-fee scams. Most would still take the free first collab because a café they know asked.
- Businesses: **11 buy · 9 pass** (identical to 11/20 without the referral model). Buyers sign up for the counter, and several said the ₹150/₹200 "don't move" them. Passes: trust 5 (up from 3: "free plus paying me ₹200 sounds too good / a catch"), convenience 3, habit 1. Still the top doubt: staff skipping PIN redemption in a rush; still asked for WhatsApp-only and in-person setup.
