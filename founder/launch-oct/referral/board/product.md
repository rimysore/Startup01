# Product lens
Score: 2/10 | Vote: PASS

## What is strong
- The instinct underneath is right: the market side is the empty side, and a local business inviting creators it already knows is a warm, believable first touch. Creators on the panel said they would still take the free first collab "because a café they know asked".
- The existing core, the customer counter, still carries the business side on its own: 11/20 businesses say yes with or without the referral money (simulated).

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **It poisons the first five minutes for the creator.** A creator gets an invite from a café, then learns the café earns ₹150 when she pays ₹499. The experience now reads as "I'm the one being sold", and matches the "pay to get a collab" scam pattern that Mitra's own offer checker is built to flag. The simulated creator panel went from 6/20 to 0/20 (same profiles); trust was the main reason.
2. **It adds three mechanics and improves none of the experience.** Revenue share, promo videos from pass holders, and a ₹200 monthly stipend are three things to explain, build and police; none makes the counter, the collab or the payment better. Businesses said the money "doesn't move" them, and the stipend raised their suspicion ("too good / a catch"). The pitch now fails the one-sentence test.
3. **Every new seam is one Mitra can't run well yet.** Payment is a manual UPI plus admin switch. Commission payouts need a registered company (not yet), PANs, TDS, likely GST advice, ASCI disclosure on posts, and anti-spam guards. Each is a hand-off where the experience breaks, and a late or disputed payout lands on the business the creator was asked to trust.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- In one sentence, what is Mitra for? Does this idea make that sentence shorter or longer?
- Describe the moment a creator opens the café's invite. What does she see, and what does she feel when she learns who gets paid?
- The panels say the counter is what brings businesses in. What problem is the referral money solving that the counter doesn't?
- Are the pass holders' promo videos paid? If not, what does that do to creators' rates?
- Who pays out the ₹150 and ₹200, through what entity, and what happens when a payout is late, reversed or disputed?
- Open: what would this cost monthly at pilot scale? No budget is stated.

## If FUND IF: the conditions
Not applicable: the vote is PASS. What would change it: the money flows from the business side or from results, never from the creator's pass; the creator panel recovers to at least its earlier level with the new pitch; and payouts wait until there is a registered company and tax advice.

## A better version of the idea, if this lens sees one (one short paragraph)
Cut all three mechanics and keep the one moment that works: the business invites creators it knows to do their free first collab through Mitra, and the reward for both sides is the result, customers counted at the counter. Make the invite effortless (a WhatsApp link, in-person setup) and the counter reliable in a rush, since staff skipping the PIN is still the top doubt. If an incentive is added later, tie it to redeemed customers, not to what a creator pays, so the story stays one sentence: "Your café brings creators, and you see exactly who walked in because of them."
