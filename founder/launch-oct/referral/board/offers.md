# Offers lens
Score: 2/10 | Vote: PASS

## What is strong
- The aim is right. The previous board said fill the market side first, and this idea is trying to do exactly that.
- Using businesses to bring in creators works: most panel creators would still take the free first collab because a café they know asked.
- The business-side offer underneath is still solid. 11 of 20 businesses say yes to the free customer counter, with or without the referral money. Their pain is real ("I give free meals to creators and have no idea if anyone walks in").

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **It hurts the creator's belief that Mitra works.** The lens says most offers fail on likelihood, and this idea attacks it head on. Creator buys went from 6/20 to 0/20, and 11 of the passes were about trust. Money now moves from the creator to the business. Public Indian scam guides call that pattern the classic registration-fee scam, the very thing Mitra's own scam check warns about.
2. **It makes the creator give up more.** Pass holders make promo videos for the businesses taking part. Creators read that as "paying to do unpaid work", and it undercuts their rates. Value falls as sacrifice rises; here the buyer pays and also works free.
3. **It adds cost and doubt but no value for businesses.** Business buys stayed at 11/20. Several said ₹150/₹200 "don't move" them, and trust passes rose from 3 to 5 ("too good / a catch"). On top of that comes ₹200 a month per business against ₹150 per paying creator, plus TDS, PAN collection, possible GST, ASCI disclosure of a material connection, and a company that does not exist yet. And the extra mostly comes down to money: Influish already pays ₹250 (50%) on the same ₹499 pass, so Mitra would be competing on the size of the reward and still offering less.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What problem stops a business from joining today that ₹150 or ₹200 solves? The panel names PIN-skipping at rush hour, WhatsApp-only setup and in-person onboarding. None of those is about money.
- How many paying creators per business do you expect, and what does one active business cost per month against what it brings in? (No budget is stated.)
- Can the business be paid at all before the company is registered and TDS/PAN handling exists?
- Is there any proof (one real paid collab, one bill-book match) that a creator could be shown before being asked for ₹499?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
Not applicable (PASS). Drop the referral share and the promo-video obligation, and the earlier FUND IF conditions apply as before.

## A better version of the idea, if this lens sees one (one short paragraph)
Pay for results, out of the side that gains, and never out of the creator's pocket. Give a business that brings in creators the thing it wants: more counted customers. For example, Mitra sets up the counter in person, works over WhatsApp, and promises that if the business's first creator post brings in fewer than an agreed number of redeemed customers in 30 days, Mitra finds it a second creator free. Reward creators who bring in other creators with free months of the pass, not with cash taken from the next creator. The invite then reads as a café sharing an opportunity, not selling a membership. A fair number for that guarantee is an open question until real redemption rates exist.
