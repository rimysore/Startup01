# Marketing: bringing businesses and brands on, without paying them from creators' passes

Evidence: `board.md` (3 pass at 2/10 on the cash referral), the panels (creators **0/20** with the referral pitch vs 6/20 without; businesses **11/20** either way, with "free *and* paying me" raising distrust), `cfo.md` (a ₹150 commission cuts contribution from ₹487 to ₹337 a pass; plan B needs ~95% more paid passes just to tie), `ops.md`, `competitors.md` (Influish pays ₹250 per pass referred). This file adds to `../marketing.md`; the base 30-day campaign there still stands.

**The idea kept:** businesses bring creators. **The idea dropped:** money from a creator's payment going to that business. Panel quotes stay in these files; none go into ads.

## 1. Positioning

**For businesses:**
1. For café and salon owners who already get creator DMs every week, Mitra is the free counter that tells you which of those creators actually brings customers: invite the ones you know, and see the count.
2. Invite your creators, keep the ones who bring customers: no cost, no commission, nothing to sell.
3. The first free collab is on us: bring a creator you trust, and we'll set up the counter at your shop.

**Recommend 1.** Panel support: all 11 business buyers said yes for the count ("I give free meals and have no idea if anyone walks in"); several said the ₹150 and ₹200 "don't move" them; trust-based passes rose when cash was added. Version 1 puts the invite inside the reason they already buy.

**For creators who arrive through a café's invite:**
1. A café you know invited you to do your first collab through Mitra. It's free, and it counts the customers you bring, so the next one pays you properly.
2. Your first collab is free. Nobody earns from your sign-up.
3. Invited by a café, counted at their counter, paid on your rate next time.

**Recommend 1**, with line 2 as a short reassurance wherever there's room. Panel support: creators' "flip" was a paid collab after the free one; their objection to the referral pitch was "the café earns from me / I'm the one being sold". "Nobody earns from your sign-up" answers that directly and sets Mitra apart from Influish's Refer & Earn.

**Against Influish's ₹250 Refer & Earn** (when a creator asks): *"Influish pays people for each creator who buys a pass. We don't: no one earns from your sign-up, and your first collab is free. Businesses invite you because they want your content, and the counter shows them what you brought."* Facts only; don't name or criticise Influish in ads.

## 3. Ten hooks for the market side

1. **Leaflet line (owner):** "Creators already DM you. Send them this link, and see which ones bring customers." *(buy reason: need)*
2. **Owner, in person:** "No cost, no commission, nothing to sell. We set it up at your counter." *(objection: "free, so what's the catch?")*
3. **Owner WhatsApp follow-up:** "Your link is ready. Share it with the next creator who asks for a free meal." *(habit: DMs from creators)*
4. **Business card on the counter:** "Creator? Ask us for our Mitra link." *(gets the invite seen)*
5. **Invite landing headline (creator):** "<Café name> invited you. Your first collab is free." *(objection: empty / who are you)*
6. **Under it:** "Nobody earns from your sign-up." *(objection: "the café earns from me")*
7. **Creator explainer line:** "Your customers are counted at their counter, so you can ask for cash next time." *(objection: cafés pay in food)*
8. **Owner results message (real numbers only):** "Your first creator brought <n> customers. Want a second?" *(buy reason: proof)*
9. **Guarantee line (owner):** "If your first post brings fewer than <n> counted customers in 30 days, we find you a second creator free." *(only once the number is set from real redemption rates)*
10. **Creator referral (later, labelled #Ad):** "Bring a creator friend: you both get a free month." *(answers "₹499 for what?" with time, not cash)*

## 5. Budget and numbers

- **No cash to businesses.** The market-side budget is rep time (already in `cfo.md`) plus ~₹5,000/month for the results guarantee once it's set.
- **Most you can pay to win a paying creator:** ₹487 contribution in year one (`cfo.md`). A free month for both sides of a creator referral costs ~₹82 of revenue in total, well under that. A ₹150 cash commission costs more and, per the panel, lowers the chance they pay at all.
- **Three numbers to watch weekly:**
  1. **Creators joining through a café link:** under 1 per live shop by L+14 → help owners share (rep sends the link with them).
  2. **First collabs done from those invites within 14 days:** under 50% → the deal room needs a nudge; call both sides.
  3. **Counted customers per first collab:** this sets the guarantee number and is the proof that brings the next business.

## Rules followed

- No cash from creators' payments to businesses; no undisclosed incentives; any creator referral reward is labelled (ASCI: #Ad, #Affiliate or #Collaboration).
- No earnings claims to creators. No fake numbers: hooks 8 and 9 use real counts only.
- Not legal advice.
