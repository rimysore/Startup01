# Competitors for the referral idea (2026-10-22)

**The idea tested:** brands, local businesses and others earn a share of each creator's ₹499/year Creator Pass when a creator they brought pays, plus free video promotion by creators, plus a small participation payment from Mitra.

Sources: public pages read on 2026-10-22 through a search tool (direct fetches were blocked in this environment). Not checked: app-store reviews of Influish's referral payouts specifically, any paid-program terms behind logins.

## 2. Price range: what a referral is worth

Share of the referred customer's payment: **lowest 2% of GMV (Kayba), median about 30% (Createntor, Subscriby's cap), highest 50% (Influish)**. On Mitra's ₹499 pass, the market range is roughly **₹100–250 per paying creator**, and Influish already pays the top of it (₹250).

## 4. What the evidence warns about (ranked)

1. **"Pay to get a collab" is the textbook creator scam in India.** Every public guide says it plainly: "Money flows from the brand to the creator. Any offer where money … flow[s] from you to them is not a brand deal." ([Kudozz](https://www.kudozz.in/blog/creator-scams-fake-brand-collaborations)); fraudsters "first demand money in the form of registration fees … or access fees to a campaign" ([Storyboard18, Aug 2025](https://www.storyboard18.com/brand-makers/scams-surge-in-creator-economy-as-fake-brands-fraud-agencies-target-influencers-78864.htm)); "They ask for a small 'registration fee.' You send ₹999. Then? They vanish." ([SITLCS, Jul 2025](https://www.sitlcs.org/post/want-to-be-a-brand-influencer-it-s-a-trapthe-new-face-of-online-job-fraud)). A café that earns when a creator it invites pays Mitra ₹499 looks exactly like this, and Mitra's own offer checker flags this pattern.
2. **Referral payouts invite spam and fake sign-ups.** The careful programs add guards: approval, tracking, holds, "only compliant creators count", clawbacks on refunds ([Lusfera](https://partners.lusfera.com/), [Subscriby](https://www.subscriby.net/blog/news/introducing-the-subscriby-partner-program)).
3. **Paid connections must be disclosed.** ASCI counts any benefit, including commissions and barter, as a "material connection" that needs a label (#Ad, #Collaboration, #Affiliate…) on the post ([ASCI guidelines](https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/guidelines/ASCI_Codes_Guidelines_Book.pdf), [ASCI FAQ](https://www.ascionline.in/social/faqs/)). A business that earns from Mitra and promotes it to creators has a material connection too.
4. **Paying commissions is a tax job.** Commission/brokerage carries TDS at 2% above an annual threshold (₹15,000 in FY 2024-25 per [TRACES](https://traces61contents.tdscpc.gov.in/en/tds-2024-2025.html); ₹20,000 under the new Act from FY 2026-27 per [TaxGuru, third party](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/tds-rates-thresholds-fy-2026-27-effective-01-04-2026-income-tax-act-2025.html)). GST treatment of the payouts is an open question for a CA. Either way it needs a registered company, PAN of each payee and payout records.

## 5. The gap

There is **no gap** in "pay people a share of the creator's pass for referrals": Influish does it at 50% with the same ₹499 pass. Copying it gives Mitra Influish's model with Influish's risks, and puts the payment on the businesses Mitra wants creators to trust.

There **is** a gap on the side nobody here covers for local businesses: **rewarding businesses for what creators earn and the customers they bring, not for what creators pay.** Creator Guard (the payer is the agency, never the creator) and FlixSell (paid on creators' earnings) show the safer shape: money flows from the business side or from results, never "pay to get a collab".

## 6. The threat

Influish could copy any referral twist overnight: it already has the referral code, the ₹499 pass and a claimed 1.5M creators. What it can't copy quickly is a counted-customer record at local counters in two Bengaluru neighbourhoods.
