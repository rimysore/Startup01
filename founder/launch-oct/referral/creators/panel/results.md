## What would flip a no

- Drop the free promo-video requirement, or pay me my rate for those videos, and show me that the businesses on Mitra pay real fees at my follower range, not just barter.
- If the promo videos were optional, or paid at my rate, and Mitra showed me real paid briefs from brands that pay what my rate card says.
- Finishing the free collab and actually getting paid on time, plus proof that Auto-DM uses Instagram's official API and won't get my account flagged.
- If my free first collab actually turned into a paid one, or a couple of creators I follow vouched that Mitra is safe and got them real paid work.
- If my free collab through Mitra turned into an actual paid collab, or Auto-DM visibly pushed my insights up, I'd pay the ₹499.
- Seeing that the free collab actually lands me a paid local collab I wouldn't have got on Influish, and being told clearly that the promo videos are optional or paid.
- If the free collab brings in real follower growth or a paid brand deal, not just a free meal, and Mitra says exactly how many promo videos the pass requires and whether they pay.
- A registered company name with a real contact and a clear rule that Mitra steps in or pays if a business doesn't pay me, plus a free collab that actually pays out on time.
- If the free collab actually got me a good collab and I could see a bunch of other local businesses on there wanting creators my size, I'd pay for the pass.
- If my free collab through Mitra turned into an actual paid collab, or the pass came with at least one guaranteed paid gig that covers the ₹499.
- If the free collab actually turned into a paid collab that came through Mitra, with a named company and a clear way to raise a complaint if a business doesn't pay, I might consider the pass after that.
- If my free first collab actually turns into a paid one through Mitra, and they show a real company name, a support contact and how they handle a business that doesn't pay.
