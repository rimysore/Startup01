# Buyer panel: Mitra (beta), creators and a customer counter for Bengaluru local businesses

**10 buy · 10 pass** (50% buy) out of 20 simulated buyers. Seed 571712, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- If my agency set it up and ran it across all my outlets and gave me the report, with no extra work for counter staff.
- If my agency set it up across all my outlets and ran it for me, and I saw a week where the WhatsApp count actually matched my bill book without my staff doing extra work.
- Another café or darshini owner nearby that I know telling me it actually showed which creator brought real people, and a creator offering to work with me only if they use it.
- A creator offering to work with me paid per customer and tracked through Mitra, so I only pay for people who actually walk in, and I can try it free without changing how my staff bill.
- Another salon or gym owner I know nearby showing me real numbers, like creators bringing in paying customers who came back, at a cost per customer below what a Google review push or a WhatsApp offer costs me.
- If I actually started paying a creator and a café owner I know showed me it counted real customers without slowing the counter.
- Another salon or gym owner I know in my area telling me they've used it for a few months and the numbers matched their bill book, or someone from Mitra coming to the shop and setting it up in person.
- If a creator my regulars already follow offered to post about my place and brought me the link themselves, and another café owner I know told me the counts matched their bill book, I'd try the free part.
- If the creator I already work with set it up for me and I saw a week of counts that matched my bill book without my staff doing anything extra at the counter.
- Another darshini or café owner nearby showing me that their customers actually showed the coupon and the evening counts matched their bill book.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
