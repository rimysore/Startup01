Mitra (beta): see how many customers each creator brings. Works on WhatsApp, Kannada and English.
A creator you're working with sends you a Mitra link on WhatsApp. Tap it, pick a 4-digit PIN, done: no app, no new login. Customers who come because of the creator's post show a coupon; your staff type the PIN on the customer's phone, or just note the code on the bill and send it later.
Every evening you get one WhatsApp message: customers counted today, from which creator, and the cost per customer. Check it against your bill book.
Free while you try it. After that, ₹299 a month only in months Mitra counts 10 or more customers for you; in a quiet month you pay nothing. Mitra takes no cut of what you pay creators. Beta: small team, Bengaluru only.
