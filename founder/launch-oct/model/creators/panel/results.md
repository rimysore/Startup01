## What would flip a no

- My first free collab gets a real count that a café owner backs up, and someone in my WhatsApp groups says a business paid them cash after seeing their Mitra record.
- If my first free counted collab shows real numbers, and I see a few Bengaluru brands paying creators because of their Mitra record, especially Kannada creators like me.
- Proof Auto-DM runs through Instagram's official API, plus a few Bengaluru creators my size using it for months with no drop in reach.
- Seeing creators my size in my WhatsApp groups actually get a paid cafe deal off their Mitra 'customers I brought' record, or the pass actually bringing me new followers.
- Proof that a brand actually paid a creator cash because of their Mitra count record. Or earning the pass free by bringing cafés, so I never pay.
- Seeing that Auto-DM actually grows my comments and followers, or a couple of Bengaluru creators my size showing they got paid cash off their Mitra record.
- Getting the pass free by bringing a café in, and then the counts actually getting me a cash collab instead of free food. Then I might pay to keep it, but only after it has paid me first.
- If my free collab actually showed real counted customers and a café paid me cash because of that record, then I'd pay the ₹499.
- My free collab actually logging real coupon redemptions, and one café paying me cash after seeing that 'customers I brought' number.
- Seeing other creators in my WhatsApp groups say a café actually paid them cash because of their Mitra customer count.
- Seeing creators from my WhatsApp groups actually get a cash collab off their Mitra counts, after a free collab where the café really used the coupon.
- If my free counted collab showed real numbers and a business paid me cash because of that record, I'd buy the pass right away.
