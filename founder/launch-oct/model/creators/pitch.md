Mitra (beta): your collabs, counted. Bengaluru, Kannada and English.
Collabs on Mitra are always free: you never pay to get or accept a collab, and Mitra never takes a cut of your fee.
Doing a collab with a café (even one you found yourself)? Send the owner your Mitra link on WhatsApp. Their customers show your coupon at the counter and every one is counted. Those counts build a "customers I brought" record on your profile that you can show the next business when you ask for cash, not food.
The Creator Pass, ₹499 a year, is for the tools: Auto-DM (comment DOSA, get the café's coupon by DM) and unlimited counted posts. Your first counted collab is free, no pass needed. Refund within 30 days if it's not worth it, no questions.
Bring a café that starts counting customers and Mitra gives you a free month of the pass. The café never pays you and you never pay the café. Beta: small team, Bengaluru only.
