# Offers lens
Score: 5/10 (for the tested offer) | Vote: FUND IF

## Which model this lens picks (1-4 or a variant), with prices and the order to switch revenue on
**Model 2, with a pay-after-paid creator pass.** Charge the side with the pain and the money. Businesses already spend free meals on creators they can't measure. Creators have the least money and the most doubts: 0/20 pay up front, 19/20 do the free counted collab.

Order:
1. **Now:** counting is free for both sides, and the creator's link is how businesses get in.
2. **After about 4 weeks of counts checked against the bill book:** ₹299/month, only in months with 10+ counted customers. Between bargain (₹199) and getting expensive (₹599); no discount.
3. **Creator Pass only after a creator's record has earned a cash collab,** which is the creators' own flip condition. ₹199–499/yr. Bringing a café still earns it free. Replace the 30-day refund with a real guarantee: no counted customers in 90 days, money back.
4. **Brands/D2C per campaign last,** once there are enough counted records to sell.

Model 3 as tested asks creators to pay before any proof: Influish's review complaint.

## What is strong
- **Value equation, business side:** a concrete outcome ("which creator brought customers"), low effort (WhatsApp only, no app or login), results on day one (the evening summary), and a no-fee quiet month that works as a built-in guarantee. Trust passes fell to 2, from 5 with the referral pitch.
- **No price competition:** Influish sells access; this sells a counted outcome.
- **Distribution is in the offer:** creators carry the link, and owners said they'd require it before giving free meals.
- **Upside the CFO didn't model:** with no rep, ₹299 is nearly all margin.

## What would kill it (top 3)
1. **Likelihood: counts read low.** Staff skipping the coupon in the rush is still the top doubt. Then businesses never reach 10, so there's no revenue and creators' records look weak. The 10+ threshold also pays owners to not redeem. Open question: the real redemption rate.
2. **The remote setup reads like a scam.** "A creator forwards a link asking for a PIN" looks like phishing to some owners (P014). With no shop visit, that first message carries all the trust.
3. **Supply stalls.** If creators don't forward links, no businesses join. Meta App Review isn't submitted, so Auto-DM (the pass's main tool) isn't live.

## How the first 20 businesses and 200 creators arrive without shop visits
- **Creators (200):** Instagram DMs and Bengaluru creator WhatsApp groups, offering "get your next barter collab counted, free". Ask every creator who joins for the cafés they already work with. Open question: cost and time per creator who signs up.
- **Businesses (20), through creators:** each creator sends their café the link. One café in ten activating would give 20, but that's an assumption to measure. Setup help is a 5-minute WhatsApp video call.
- **Proof loop:** the first outlets share bill-book-matched counts, and that becomes the opening line of later links ("Café X, Jayanagar: 14 counted last month").
- **One neighbourhood,** so owners can vouch for each other.

## Conditions
- [ ] No up-front Creator Pass at launch. It switches on only after a record has earned a cash collab.
- [ ] Measure coupon redemption against bill books at 3–5 outlets before charging ₹299.
- [ ] Remove the reason to undercount: consider a results guarantee (a free month if counts fall below an agreed number) instead of the 10+ threshold.
- [ ] The link names the company and a reachable person. Register the company before taking money.
- [ ] Track: share of creators who forward a link, share of cafés that activate, cost per business. Review at 20 businesses.
- [ ] Submit Meta App Review now. The counter must work without it.
