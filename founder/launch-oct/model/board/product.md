# Product lens
Score: 5/10 (for the tested offer) | Vote: FUND IF

## Which model this lens picks (1-4 or a variant), with prices and the order to switch revenue on
**Model 2, simplified.** One sentence: "Mitra tells a Bengaluru café which creator brought customers in, on WhatsApp." The tested offer (model 3) needs two prices and a paragraph per side, and the creator half sold 0/20: cut it.
1. **Launch:** creators free, no pass. Businesses free while trying (trial length is an open question).
2. **After the trial:** businesses ₹299/month, only in months with 10+ counted customers. Between the bargain (₹199) and getting-expensive (₹599) medians; 10/20 owners said yes.
3. **Once Meta approves Auto-DM and creators can point to cash collabs:** an optional tools pass at about ₹199/yr (bargain median), free for bringing a café that counts.
4. **Later still:** brands pay per campaign (model 4), sold on counted records that don't exist yet. Price open.

## What is strong
- **The creator is the installer.** The café gets set up from a link the creator sends; no rep or app needed. 19/20 creators would do the free counted collab, and several owners said they would make "send a Mitra link" a condition of free meals.
- **The business side fits in one WhatsApp thread:** tap, PIN, an evening summary. The first panel asked for exactly this.
- **Trust passes on the business side fell from 5 to 2** once the cash-referral money was gone and pricing became "pay only when it works".

## What would kill it (top 3)
1. **Counts read low, and now nobody from Mitra is at the till.** The top doubt in every panel is still staff skipping the PIN at rush hour. The first board's fix, in-person setup, is ruled out. Worse, paying only above 10 counts gives owners a reason not to count. Nobody knows the real counted share.
2. **The seam at the hand-off.** A creator's WhatsApp link asking for a PIN looked like a scam to at least one owner. If the first collab counts two coupons, both sides give up and Mitra was never in the room.
3. **Too many things for zero users:** a marketplace, Auto-DM (Meta app not set up), a pass with manual UPI, a referral month and a business plan, all at once. Auto-DM at ₹499 is Influish's offer.

## How the first 20 businesses and 200 creators arrive without shop visits
- **Creators (200):** reach them remotely in the creator WhatsApp groups and through Instagram DMs with one offer: "your next barter collab, counted, free". Start with barter creators in the two neighbourhoods. Cost per sign-up is untested.
- **Businesses (20):** each creator sends the link to a café they already work with. Mitra follows up the same day from a named WhatsApp Business number: a Kannada voice note, a 10-minute video call with the owner and one staff member, and a day-3 check against the bill book. Agencies as a channel (two owners named theirs) is an open question.
- The deciding open question: what share of creator links become a counting café. Measure from week one.

## Conditions
1. Launch with one sentence, one price (business) and no creator pass.
2. Make it quicker to count than to skip: the "code on the bill" route as the default and a remote bill-book check in week one. Publish the counted share.
3. Price on customers, not on staff effort: test whether a "10+ counted" threshold makes owners count less (e.g. a flat ₹299 after a proven trial month).
4. Every link and summary comes from one named, registered sender with a reachable person; register the company before the first ₹299.
5. Each revenue step switches on only when its proof exists (counts → business price; cash collabs and Meta approval → creator pass; counted records → brands).
