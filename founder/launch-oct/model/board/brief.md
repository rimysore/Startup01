# Board brief: choosing Mitra's business model (October 2026)

Background (read first): /home/user/Startup01/founder/launch-oct/board/brief.md (what Mitra is and what's built), /home/user/Startup01/founder/launch-oct/board.md (first board: lead with the customer counter), /home/user/Startup01/founder/launch-oct/referral/board.md (cash referrals to businesses: 3 pass) and referral/cfo.md (year-1 numbers).

## The founder's situation
- Stuck on the business model. **New hard constraint: the founder cannot do shop-to-shop visits in Bengaluru** (founder is in the USA; no field team). Anything that needs a rep walking into shops is out.
- Wants a model that is efficient (no field force) and attractive to both creators and businesses.

## The offer tested today (founder/launch-oct/model/*/pitch.md)
- **Creators:** collabs always free (never pay to get or accept a collab; no cut of fees). Creators send a café their Mitra link on WhatsApp for any collab, even ones they found themselves; each customer who shows the coupon is counted and builds a "customers I brought" record for the creator to show the next business. Creator Pass ₹499/yr = tools (Auto-DM, unlimited counted posts); first counted collab free; 30-day refund. Bring a café that starts counting → creator gets a free pass month (Mitra pays; café never pays the creator, creator never pays the café).
- **Businesses:** WhatsApp-only setup from the creator's link (tap, pick a 4-digit PIN; no app, no login); staff type the PIN on the customer's phone or note the code on the bill; one WhatsApp summary every evening (customers today, by creator, cost per customer). Free while trying; then ₹299/month only in months Mitra counts 10+ customers; nothing in quiet months.

## Results (simulated panels, same buyer cards as the first panel; buy rates are an upper bound)
- **Creators: 0/20 would pay ₹499 up front, but 19/20 would do the free counted collab.** Pass reasons: trust 11, need 7, habit 2. Repeated: "I'll pay once a café pays me cash because of my count"; doubts café staff will check coupons; Auto-DM account-flag fear; Influish same price with more creators. Median price answers (₹/year): too cheap 49, bargain 199, getting expensive 499, too expensive 999.
- **Businesses: 10/20 sign up and would pay ₹299/month in months it counts 10+** (first panel: 11/20 for a free tool with in-person setup; referral pitch 11/20). Passes: need 4 (don't use creators at all; not the target), convenience 3 (no time for PIN in rush), trust 2 (down from 5 with the cash-referral pitch). Several owners volunteered they'd make "send a Mitra link" a condition of giving free meals to creators. Still the top doubt everywhere: staff/customers skipping the coupon in a rush, making counts low. Median price answers (₹/month): too cheap 49, bargain 199, getting expensive 599, too expensive 1,100.

## Other evidence
- Influish: creators pay ₹499/yr for access + Auto-DM; pays ₹250 per referred pass; claims 1.5M creators. Its worst reviews: paid and got no collabs.
- CFO (referral/cfo.md, estimates): with no paid rep, a creator-pass-only model is about break-even-to-small-profit in year 1 if ~490 passes sell; every plan loses money with a ₹25k/month rep. Business revenue was not modelled before; ₹299/month × paying businesses is new.

## The question for the board
Which business model should Mitra launch with, given no field force? Score the offer above and say what to change. Options the founder is weighing:
1. Creators pay (₹499/yr pass), businesses free.
2. Businesses pay after proof (₹299/month in months with 10+ counted customers), creators free (tools paid later or cheaper).
3. Both, as tested: creators pay for tools, businesses pay after proof.
4. Brands/D2C pay per campaign (remote-friendly, bigger budgets), local businesses and creators free.
Name the model, the prices, the order to switch revenue on, and how the first 20 businesses and 200 creators arrive without shop visits.
