# Monopoly lens
Score: 5/10 (for the tested offer) | Vote: FUND IF

## Which model this lens picks (1-4 or a variant), with prices and the order to switch revenue on
**Model 2, with model 4 as the expansion.** Businesses pay after proof; creators never pay at launch; brands later buy from the counted record.
1. **Now:** free for everyone in one neighbourhood. Creator Pass is earned (free months for bringing a counting café), not sold.
2. **Once the counter is trusted there:** switch on ₹299/month for businesses in months with 10+ counted customers (inside the panel's ₹199-599 band).
3. **After creators get cash collabs from their records:** offer the pass at about ₹199/yr (the creator panel's bargain point), not ₹499 (0/20 would pay ₹499; that is Influish's offer).
4. **Then brands/D2C** pay per campaign, priced per counted customer. Only Mitra holds that record.

## What is strong
- **Distribution is built into the product.** The creator's WhatsApp link is how the café joins. 19/20 creators would do the free counted collab. Several owners said they would make "send a Mitra link" a condition of free meals. Each side recruits the other, no rep.
- **The right side pays.** Businesses carry the measurement pain. 10/20 would pay. Trust-based passes fell to 2 from 5 under the referral pitch.
- **A possible proprietary asset.** A creator's "customers I brought" record, checked against bill books, is data Influish's public material does not describe. It compounds with every counted collab.
- **"Never pay to get a collab"** separates Mitra from the registration-fee scam and Influish's worst reviews.

## What would kill it (top 3)
1. **Counts read low, and the price rewards it.** Staff skipping the coupon in a rush was the top doubt everywhere. Under a 10+ trigger, a café that undercounts also stays free. Nobody is at the till to fix it, and low counts sink creators' records too.
2. **No density.** Cafés that arrive by creator links land wherever creators eat, scattered across the city. The network effect only works if many counting cafés sit close together.
3. **Thin revenue and a copyable feature.** Hand calculation from the CFO's no-rep fixed costs (₹9,667/month): breaking even on the business fee alone needs about 33 cafés paying ₹299 in the same month. Influish, which claims 1.5M creators, could add a coupon counter. Record and density are the only defences; both are at zero.

## How the first 20 businesses and 200 creators arrive without shop visits
- **Pick one neighbourhood.** Recruit creators who post there first. None is named yet.
- **200 creators:** remote Instagram DMs and WhatsApp-group posts offering a free counted collab to creators already doing café barter. Earned pass months reward each café a creator brings. Cost per creator is an open question.
- **20 businesses:** they come in through those creators' links. A founder WhatsApp video call sets up each of the first cafés remotely and compares the evening summary with the bill book.
- **Proof travels by WhatsApp.** Each counting café's owner gets a shareable bill-book match to send nearby owners.
- Open question: the background brief mentions "people on the ground in India". What can they do short of shop visits?

## Conditions
1. Name the neighbourhood and a target number of counting cafés there.
2. Measure the real redemption rate against bill books at the first cafés before ₹299 switches on.
3. Stop the trigger rewarding undercounting. For example, frame the fee as "cost per counted customer" so fuller counts look good.
4. No paid Creator Pass at launch. Relaunch it at about ₹199 only after creators report cash collabs won from their records.
5. Build the brand/D2C offer on the record only once records exist in the chosen neighbourhood.
6. Before billing anyone: register the company, name a reachable operator and start Meta App Review. The counter must work without Auto-DM.
