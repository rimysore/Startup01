## The stack
- **Core:** businesses see exactly which creator brought which customer, on WhatsApp, with no app. Creators turn barter into a record that wins cash collabs.
- **Bonuses:**
  - remote setup by video;
  - a bill-book match;
  - a free pass month for a creator who brings a café that counts.
- **Guarantee:**
  - businesses pay nothing in a month under 10 counted customers;
  - the creator pass has a 90-day refund if the creator gets no counted customers.
- **Urgency:** the launch month is free (true: FREE_UNTIL).
