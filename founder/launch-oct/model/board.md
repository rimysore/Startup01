**Verdict: 3 of 3 FUND IF, and all three picked the same model.** That is up from 4/10 on the first board and 2/10 on cash referrals.

## The model
1. **Businesses pay after proof.** They are free while trying. After that, a business pays ₹299 a month only in months with 10+ counted customers. Billing starts only after 4 weeks of counts have been checked against the bill book.
2. **Creators are free.** Collabs are always free, and creators build a "customers I brought" record.
3. **Creator tools come later.** A pass of about ₹199 a year (not ₹499, which is Influish's offer) goes on sale once Auto-DM is Meta-approved and a creator has won a cash collab from their record.
4. **Brands and D2C come last.** They pay per campaign, priced per counted customer, once records exist.

## The same top 3 risks in all three memos
1. **Counts read low.** Staff skip coupons at rush time, and the "pay at 10+" rule gives owners a reason to undercount. Fix: code on the bill rather than a PIN, plus a bill-book match. Consider a flat fee after the trial in place of the threshold.
2. **A WhatsApp link from a creator asking for a PIN looks like phishing.** Fix: a named Mitra WhatsApp number, a Kannada voice note, and a 10-minute video call to set up.
3. **Cafés end up scattered across the city.** Fix: seed creators who already post in one neighbourhood first.

## Getting users with no field force
- **Creators:** reach 200 through Instagram DMs and Bengaluru creator WhatsApp groups with one line: "your next barter collab, counted, free".
- **Cafés:** each creator sends the link to cafés they already work with. If about 1 in 10 activates, that is about 20 businesses; this rate must be measured. The founder sets each up remotely.
- **Owner pull:** owners can make "send a Mitra link" a condition of giving a free meal to a creator.

## Conditions, merged
- One neighbourhood.
- Measure: redemption rate, link-to-café activation, cost per creator.
- About 33 paying cafés in the same month break even on the business fee alone (Monopoly memo, from CFO fixed costs).
- Nothing changes in code until the founder decides. Meta App Review is still the founder's step.
