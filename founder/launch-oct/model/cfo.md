# CFO: Mitra year 1 with business revenue (remote-first, no paid rep)

Tool output: `cfo-D.md` (inputs `numbers-D.json`). The combined and flat-fee rows below are hand calculations from the same inputs. Amounts are in ₹, before GST. **Every input except the prices is an estimate: there are no real sales yet.** This is not financial, tax or legal advice. An accountant should check GST on the fee and the company structure.

## Assumptions
- **Unit:** one paying business-month at ₹299. Contribution is ₹288 after Razorpay (₹7) and the WhatsApp evening summary (~₹4).
- **Fixed costs: ₹10,167 a month.**
  - No rep and no shop visits.
  - Hosting ₹3,000, compliance ₹1,667, creator outreach ₹5,000, WhatsApp number ₹500.
- **Startup:** company registration ₹15,000.
- **Ramp (an assumption, one neighbourhood growing to a few):**
  - No paying cafés in months 1–2: the free launch month plus the 4-week proof.
  - Then 5, 10, 15, 20, 26, 32, 38, 44, 50, 56 paying cafés.
  - That is about 296 paying café-months in year 1.
- **Creator pass:** ₹199 from month 7, after Meta approval and the first cash collabs. 10 passes in month 7, rising to 35 in month 12, so 135 in total. Contribution is ₹194 each.

## The CFO's note
- **The margin.** Each paying café-month leaves ₹288 of ₹299 (96%). At 56 paying cafés, Mitra makes about ₹6,000 a month after all costs.
- **The line to watch: paying cafés.** Fixed costs are tiny without a rep, so this is the only number that matters. Break-even is 36 cafés paying in the same month, and every 10 cafés is about ₹2,900 a month.
  - That depends on two unmeasured rates: how many creator links become counting cafés, and how many cafés reach 10+ counted customers.
  - Halve the cafés and year 1 loses about ₹79,000.
- **Cash.** About ₹65,000 covers the whole first year at this ramp, compared with ₹2 lakh with a paid rep. Year 1 does not earn back the ₹15,000 startup spend, but the business runs profitably each month from month 9 (month 8 with the pass).
- **Don't cut the business price to ₹199.** At ₹199, break-even moves from 36 to 54 cafés and year 1 is ₹30,000 worse.
  - The panel's "bargain" median was ₹199, but ₹299 is below the "getting expensive" median of ₹599.
  - Keep ₹299, and test ₹199 only if real cafés refuse.
- **About the threshold:**
  - On these numbers, "pay only in months with 10+" (₹299) earns about the same as a flat ₹199 for every active café, and needs 18 fewer cafés.
  - Keep the threshold: it is the guarantee that gets cafés to say yes.
  - Watch for undercounting with the week-1 bill-book check.

### Three ways to improve it
1. **More paying cafés per creator:** this is the biggest lever. +20% cafés improves year 1 by about ₹17,000.
2. **Add the ₹199 creator pass in month 7:** +₹26,190, taking year 1 from -₹36,736 to about -₹10,566.
3. **Spend outreach only where cafés activate:** the ₹5,000 a month is 49% of fixed costs. Halving it once the first neighbourhood works saves ₹30,000 a year.

### Is plan D worse than A without a rep?
That figure relies on ~492 creators paying ₹499, and the panels say 0 of 20 would pay up front. Plan D's revenue comes from the side that said it would pay (10 of 20 cafés), so its smaller number is more believable.
