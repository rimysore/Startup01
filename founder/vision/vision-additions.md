# Founder's additions to the roadmap (21 Oct 2026)

1. **Earn through affiliates.** Mitra helps creators earn through affiliate offers matched to their audience (roadmap Phase 5), not only sponsorships.
2. **Local-business matching agent.** An agent continuously searches the internet for businesses near the creator and relevant to their audience (local shops, restaurants, coaching centres, clinics, real estate, regional brands), and matches creator ↔ business on both sides: the business gets a creator whose audience is local and right for it; the creator gets paid local deals and affiliate-style arrangements. Mitra prepares the pitch to the business; nothing is sent without the creator's approval.
3. **Negotiation.** Mitra helps creators negotiate: analyses an offer, suggests a fair rate from the creator's real performance and past deals, flags missing terms (deliverables, usage rights, exclusivity, payment dates), drafts counter-offers for the creator to approve.
4. **"AI CMO for every creator."** Each creator gets a manager that knows their channel, content and goals from their own data, and brands/businesses can come in to find and work with creators: a complete ecosystem.
