## What would flip a no

- Seeing that the weekly ideas beat my usual views on my own channel, and that Mitra only ever shows a deal I can say no to, with the fee visible before I accept.
- A free month where Mitra's title or hook beat my usual views on a real video, and I could see that in my own numbers, with no sponsored or affiliate content forced into my uploads.
- A known company or creator I trust vouches for it, or I can use the free tier for a few weeks and see it give me a title or thumbnail that clearly beats my usual views.
- A free trial on one of my own videos that shows it beat my usual views, so I can see the result before I pay anything.
- A free trial that shows it beat my usual views on a real video, plus a written promise that nothing goes to any business without my explicit OK.
- Seeing proof on my own channel that Mitra's suggestions beat my usual views, or a free tier that is useful enough on its own to show that before I pay.
- Mitra finds me a real local deal, like a shop or coaching centre paying me for a video, and it shows that the deal earned more than my usual weeks of ad revenue. One proven paid deal would matter more to me than any feature list.
- Seeing a real example from a channel my size where the weekly plan beat its usual views, plus a free trial that doesn't need a card.
- A clear, proven example from a creator with my kind of channel showing Mitra's advice beat their usual views by a real margin, shown for free before I pay anything.
- A free week where I can see Mitra's plan for my own channel and approve or reject every single brand message before anything goes out, with the fee stated up front.
- A free trial that shows, on my own channel's numbers, a title or script that beat my usual views.
- Seeing that it actually beat my usual views on my own channel, with a free trial that shows the result before I pay, and a clear promise that affiliate picks never go out without my OK.
