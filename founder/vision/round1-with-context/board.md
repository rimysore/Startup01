# Board summary: Mitra's new vision (21 Oct 2026)

**Vote: 2 FUND IF · 1 PASS. Average 3.7 / 10** (Offers 4, Product 4, Monopoly 3). Same average as the first board on the analytics product: the vision is bigger, but the evidence under it hasn't changed yet.

## What all three like
- Selling the outcome ("more views, then more income") instead of a dashboard.
- Phase 1's experience: one "Today" card with 1–3 actions, each with a reason, measured against the creator's own baseline. The Retention Sprint is already its first version.
- The discipline: gates, "do not build yet", no new page unless it's a repeated workflow, managed campaigns before marketplace code, density in one language + niche.

## Risks more than one member raised
1. **No proof yet, and connection is the bottleneck.** (all) No real connected channel, no payer; about half the simulated "yes" creators only want the free WhatsApp check. The Creator Brain has nothing to learn from until creators connect.
2. **YouTube owns the ground and the rules.** (all) Free Ask Studio may absorb Phases 1–3; OAuth verification pending, Analytics amendment not submitted; the 30-day refresh-or-delete rule cuts against "persistent memory"; no sharing API data with third parties (brands) without consent.
3. **The money sits with brands, six phases away.** (Offers, Monopoly) Creator SaaS alone is small and price-sensitive (760 payers at ₹299 to cover a salary).
4. **"AI CMO with brands logging in" sprawls.** (Product, Monopoly) From Phase 4 the manager serves two masters (creator and paying brand); each later phase is a crowded category entered without a lead.
5. **Measurement may be noise** for small, irregular channels. (Product)

## How they'd reorder it
- **Phase 0: data rights.** Written read of the YouTube API policy + consent design: what can be stored past 30 days and shown to brands. (Monopoly, Product)
- **Phase 1a, now:** Retention Sprint as the "Today" card, one action per upload with the rewritten title/hook ready to paste, for the 30 beta creators. Creator DNA and the weekly plan later. (Product, Offers)
- **Pull forward by hand:** a media kit + rate check for creators with brand interest (Offers), and one manually run brand campaign for 10–20 Kannada creators in one niche once a handful are connected (Monopoly). Test the manager offer early (Offers).
- **Shrink or merge** Phases 2, 3 and 8; treat 4–8 as hypotheses; keep "brands log in" out of anything a creator sees.

## Conditions (merged)
- [ ] ≥15 of 30 beta creators connect and get a weekly "Today" card; ≥half accept an action that reaches a published video. (Product)
- [ ] ≥5 documented before/after results on real channels, counting "inconclusive" honestly. (all)
- [ ] ≥10 creators or managers pay a real price; test above ₹299 for the AI-manager offer. (Offers)
- [ ] OAuth verification + Analytics amendment approved, or a written fallback; 30-day rule designed into the Brain. (all)
- [ ] An offer name (not "Video Doctor") and an affordable guarantee. (Offers)
- [ ] One brand pays for one manually run campaign through a creator pool, before any brand-side code. (Monopoly: this flips PASS)
