# Mitra: the new vision, for a board review

Read the full roadmap in ../roadmap.md (the founder's document). Summary and context below.

## The vision
"The AI growth and business manager for regional-language (Indian) creators." YouTube analytics is the wedge.
Eight gated phases: (1) Creator Brain + Growth Autopilot: persistent creator-specific memory, 1–3 weekly actions with reasons, recommendation lifecycle proposed → accepted → published → measured against the creator's baseline; (2) Mitra Studio: content packs (hooks, script, titles, thumbnails, description, Shorts ideas) per approved action; (3) Repurpose + approved distribution (YouTube, then Instagram); (4) Mitra Earn v1: live media kit, sponsorship rate guidance, brand-deal CRM, negotiation help; (5) Affiliate layer via existing networks; (6) Managed brand campaigns (service first: creator pools by language + niche, outbound to brands/agencies, transparent fee); (7) Two-sided marketplace + "Regional Creator Graph" (creator ↔ audience ↔ content ↔ brand ↔ commerce outcomes); (8) Creator Business OS (Today/Create/Grow/Earn, team accounts for managers, automation policies).
Principles: outcome-first; one manager, many internal agents; creator-specific intelligence over generic generation; regional-native not translated; approval before any external action; measure outcomes; no new page unless it is a repeated workflow. Each phase has a gate and a "do not build yet" list. Revenue model: recurring creator software + transaction revenue (brand and affiliate commerce), "earned only when Mitra delivers value". The founder also frames it as "an AI CMO for each influencer, trained on their own data, with brands logging in: a full ecosystem".

## Where Mitra is today (21 Oct 2026)
- Live free beta at trymitraapp.com: Hindi and Kannada only; Video Doctor + Title & Thumbnail Studio only (other features paused); no prices shown; 100 free credits; Retention Sprint (3 fixes per upload over 4 uploads, average % viewed before vs after) and a Progress page; first-party beta events, output ratings, an in-app price survey. Not yet tested with a real connected channel. Beta recruiting starts 2 Nov via creator WhatsApp groups and managers, budget under ₹25K; goal 30 creators, 5–10 documented results.
- Traction: 0 paying customers; a handful of testers. Google OAuth verification pending; the YouTube API "Analytics & Reporting" amendment (needed for Mitra's own derived scores) not yet submitted. YouTube API policy: authorized data must be refreshed or deleted after 30 days; API data can't be sold or shared with third parties without consent.
- Team: two founders, one in the US (engineering/product, Eastern time) and one part-time in India (marketing, outreach, creator conversations, IST). Bootstrapped.
- Earlier evidence (all simulated, not real customers): first board on the original analytics product scored 3.7/10 (2 FUND IF, 1 PASS): platform dependence, no proof, no distribution, price as the pitch. Simulated creator panels: old ₹99 analytics pitch 2/20 would buy; free beta 11/20; a "Kannada & Hindi Retention Sprint" offer (free founder WhatsApp check of title, thumbnail and first 15 seconds before connecting; trust card; group call; 1:1 review if no improvement; 40% off for a year after) 15/20, though about half of those only commit to the free WhatsApp check, not to connecting their channel. Planned price after beta: ₹299/month creators, ~₹999 managers. CFO: ~$3.59 (86%) contribution per paying month; break-even 760 payers with a $2K/month founder salary, 203 without.
- Competitive notes from earlier research: YouTube's free Ask Studio reads the channel and may support Indian languages; vidIQ/TubeBuddy/ViewStats are English-first and priced higher; VidUp (Indian) does pre-upload titles/hooks; no one found doing own-channel diagnosis + correct Indic thumbnail text. The name "Video Doctor" is already used by Virexa AI. Indian influencer-marketing platforms and global creator-business tools (media kits, deal CRMs, marketplaces) exist at the later phases; their exact coverage of regional-language creators was not researched.
