# Mitra: the vision, for a board review

Judge the VISION and PLAN on their own merits: the idea, the market logic, the phase order and the business model. Read the founder's roadmap in full: /home/user/Startup01/founder/vision/roadmap.md, and the founder's additions: /home/user/Startup01/founder/vision/vision-additions.md.

In one line: Mitra is the AI growth and business manager ("an AI CMO") for India's regional-language creators. It learns each creator's channel, content and audience from their own connected data, tells them the 1–3 things worth doing each week and measures whether it worked, prepares the content, helps them publish, and then helps them earn: sponsorships, affiliate offers, and paid deals with nearby local businesses that an agent finds online and matches to the creator, with help negotiating. Brands and businesses come in to find creators by real audience performance, not follower counts, building a regional creator graph. YouTube first, other platforms later. Revenue: creator software subscriptions plus transaction revenue on brand, local and affiliate deals.

Stage: an early product exists (YouTube analytics, video diagnosis, title/thumbnail help) with two founders; the vision above is the plan from here. Do not score the current traction; score whether this vision and this plan are right, what would make them fail, and how you would change the plan.
