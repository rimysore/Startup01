# Buyer panel: Mitra, a free app for Bengaluru creators: paid local deals plus a scam and terms check for every offer you get

**10 buy · 10 pass** (50% buy) out of 20 simulated buyers. Seed 2110, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- A friend showing me a real paid invite they got through Mitra from a place near me, with the fee actually paid.
- If it worked fully inside WhatsApp, where I just forward a DM and get the check back, and if the businesses on it showed fees at or above my usual rate, I'd try it.
- A creator I follow in Bengaluru showing a real café or gym deal they got through it, with the fee, and me being able to just forward a DM on WhatsApp without installing anything.
- Seeing a few creators my size in Bengaluru actually getting paid invites through it that were higher than what those places offered them directly.
- Someone in my WhatsApp group posting that they got a real paid invite from a Bengaluru café through Mitra at a fee as good as what the agency gets me.
- A creator my size in Bengaluru showing me a real paid invite with the fee written down that they got through Mitra, or a friend sending me one.
- Seeing real paid invites with the fee written down from places in my area, e.g. a friend with my follower count getting a few paid deals through it in a month.
- If it worked right inside WhatsApp, and a friend showed me she actually got a paid café or salon deal through it with the fee written down.
- If Mitra held the business's money upfront and released it to me the day the post goes live, I'd turn on local invites.
- A creator I actually know in Bengaluru telling me they got a real paid café or salon deal through it, with the fee paid on time.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
