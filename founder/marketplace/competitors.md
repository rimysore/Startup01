## 2. Price range

**Comparable item: what a business pays an intermediary for one ~₹5,000 local creator deal, on top of the creator fee.**

| Option | Cost on a ₹5,000 deal |
|---|---|
| Hypee, Influish (stated), Localyse free tier, Instagram creator marketplace, direct DM | ₹0 |
| Restronaut (10%) | ₹500 |
| *Mitra (10%, + GST)* | *₹500 + ₹90* |
| OPA (nano Instagram, per creator) | ₹700-1,000, but tiers start at 100 creators |
| CollabVue campaign post | ₹997 (or ₹12-36 to reveal 1-3 contacts) |
| GCC/Winkl managed (third-party claim, 15-25%) | ₹750-1,250 |
| Branditars seeding (₹50,000 / up to 20 creators) | ≥₹2,500 per creator |

- **Lowest:** ₹0 (five options). **Median of the competitors listed:** ₹500. **Highest per deal:** ~₹2,500 (agency). By total ticket, Spettro's ~₹1 lakh campaign minimum is the highest.
- **Creator side:** most platforms are free. Influish charges ₹499/year to unlock paid campaigns, Restronaut 12%, and agencies about 20% ("These agencies take 20% of the fee brands offer", [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/brand-makers/scams-surge-in-creator-economy-as-fake-brands-fraud-agencies-target-influencers-78864.htm)). Every scam check found is free.
- **Creator fee benchmark (Bengaluru):** nano creators take barter or ₹1,200-3,000, micro ₹3,500-10,000, and Kannada creators cost 20-35% less than English-first ones ([Spettro](https://spettro.in/blog/influencer-marketing-agency-bengaluru)).
- **Paid-ads benchmark:** a ₹750 Instagram boost test; Zomato ₹15-40k/month; Google ₹10-25k/month for a café.

Mitra's 10% sits at the median. It is under every managed option, but above the free self-serve tools and direct DMs, which are where a small café starts.

## 5. The gap

Nobody combines all four of these for a Bengaluru café or salon:
- (a) neighbourhood-level, Kannada-capable creator matching,
- (b) a per-deal fee sized for a ₹5,000-50,000 budget rather than a ₹50k-1 lakh agency minimum,
- (c) customers counted at the counter (promo code + tally),
- (d) for creators, a free check on every incoming offer plus a guarantee that they never pay to get work.

The evidence:
- The most-used creator app makes creators pay ₹499 to apply. "Paid, got nothing" is its top complaint (6 of the reviews read), and a large share of its campaigns are barter.
- Platform non-payment is the top creator fear across GCC, OPA, Hobo.Video and agencies.
- The free self-serve tools (CollabVue, Hypee, Localyse, Instagram's marketplace) stop at discovery. The business still negotiates, chases and measures alone, and none of them measures walk-ins.
- Bengaluru agencies do local and Kannada work but start at ₹50,000-1 lakh ([Branditars](https://branditars.com/services/influencer-marketing), [Spettro](https://spettro.in/blog/influencer-marketing-agency-bengaluru)).
- Restaurants distrust both the "influencers" (TOI) and per-click ads that don't convert (MediaNama). That makes "cost per customer counted" a message no listed competitor offers.
- Scam checkers exist but are English and US-framed. None is free, Kannada and built into a deal inbox.

**Caveats:**
- The gap is narrow on price. Several options are ₹0, so Mitra's 10% has to be justified by matching, results and trust, not by being cheaper.
- The whole category is crowded with small new entrants (CollabVue, Hypee, Localyse, Restronaut, Crafted) launched in 2025-26. None shows traction yet, and neither does Mitra.
- Whether Bengaluru owners will pay anything above barter has not been measured. That is still the biggest unknown.

## 6. The threat

- **Fastest copier: Influish.** It already has 1.5M+ creators, brand self-serve, escrow and a fresh pre-seed at a ₹25 crore valuation ([ET, 17 Jun 2026](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/funding/creator-platform-influish-raises-pre-seed-round-at-rs-25-crore-valuation/articleshow/131778800.cms)), and it says it is building "an agency dashboard with escrow payments, automated contracts". A city filter, Kannada and a scam-check feature are weeks of work for it. Its weakness is that it charges creators, and Mitra's free-for-creators promise is the direct counter.
- **Crafted** is in the same city on the same restaurant wedge, and its founder is publicly working out pricing now.
- **Meta:** Instagram's creator marketplace is free, is folding into a Meta Creator Marketing Hub "later in 2026" ([NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/meta-to-unify-creator-marketplace-and-partnership-ads-hub-adds-facebook-creators-to-roster/)), and is already used for regional targeting ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/news/instagram-creator-marketplace-free-data-saas-influencer-marketing-platforms-brands-matchmaker-youtube-ai-11758695974975.html)). If it adds local SMB tooling, discovery becomes free for everyone.
- **CollabVue** could add a Bengaluru push and a per-deal fee overnight.

The defences that are hard to copy:
- verified Bengaluru creator density in Kannada,
- counter-counted outcome data per neighbourhood and niche,
- the "creators never pay" trust position, held from day one.
