# Competitive landscape (checked 2026-10-08)

**Question:** is anyone already building an AI layer that connects a campus's facilities tools (AiM, e-Builder, finance, condition assessments, Excel) and gives leadership cross-system insights and reports?

**Short answer:** the space is **not empty, but the exact spot is open for now.** Every big vendor is adding AI **inside its own product**. I found no product positioned as a **vendor-neutral analyst across all of a campus's systems**, built for higher-ed capital planning and board reporting. The window is real but probably short, roughly 12–24 months (judgment, not data).

This is based on web searches. It isn't exhaustive, and small startups or recent announcements can be missed. Confirm in interviews.

## What this means for us

1. **Don't pitch "AI for facilities."** That's crowded and every vendor will claim it.
2. **Pitch the neutral layer:** "Your vendors' AI sees only their own system. Leadership's questions cross all of them." This also answers your observation that AiM's assistant will stay inside AiM.
3. **Higher ed is the specific wedge.** Board reports, state capital requests, Gordian/Sightlines FCI data, APPA-style KPIs, the July fiscal year. Generic tools (Procore, Power BI) don't ship these.
4. **Vendor AI and MCP help us.** As AiM, Accruent and others expose data to AI agents, connecting to them gets cheaper. We should plan to connect through those interfaces, not fight them.
5. **Beat DIY on trust and time-to-value.** Reconciled numbers with citations, a first board report in 2 weeks, no IT project. A Power BI build takes months and still doesn't write the narrative.
6. **Biggest risk: Procore.** If higher-ed owners standardize on Procore's owner suite, it covers part of our value. Mitigation: support Procore as one more source, and win on cross-system scope and higher-ed reporting.

## Moat to build (in order)
1. A higher-ed facilities **data model** and connectors across AiM, e-Builder, Procore, Gordian exports and finance systems
2. **Trust layer:** reconciliation checks and source citations for every number
3. **Library of report templates and KPIs** from design partners (board reports, state submissions)
4. Relationships and references in the higher-ed facilities community

## Add these to the interview guide
- "Has your AiM / e-Builder / Gordian rep shown you AI features? What would they still not answer?"
- "Has your IT team tried Power BI or Copilot over your facilities data? How far did it get?"
- "Would you trust one vendor's AI to report on data from its competitors' systems?"
