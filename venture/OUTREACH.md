# Interview outreach: facilities directors & capital planners

Goal: 20 conversations with facilities and capital-planning leaders at **other** institutions.
Before sending: check your employer's outside-activity policy, use a personal email address, and don't name your employer in outreach until that's settled.

**Rules**
- Ask for advice, not a sale. No pitch, no price, no attachment in the first email.
- Keep it under 120 words. Make one specific, easy ask: 20 minutes.
- Personalize line 1 with something public: their capital plan, a board presentation, an APPA talk, a recent project.
- Send Tuesday–Thursday mornings. Follow up twice, 4–5 days apart, then stop.
- Track every send in a simple sheet: name, institution, type, date sent, follow-ups, reply, interview date.

---

## 1. Cold email (main template)

**Subject options**
- Question about your capital planning reporting
- 20 minutes on board reporting at [Institution]?
- How [Institution] puts together its capital plan

```
Hi [First name],

I read [Institution]'s [FY26–30 capital plan / recent board presentation on deferred maintenance] and [one specific, genuine observation, e.g. "the way you tie projects to FCI by building was really clear"].

I work in capital planning and facilities at a large public university, and I'm researching how facilities teams pull together board reports, forecasts and efficiency data across systems like AiM, e-Builder and Excel.

Would you be open to a 20-minute call in the next two weeks? I'm not selling anything. I'd like to learn how your team handles it and what's hardest. Happy to share what I'm hearing from other campuses in return.

Thanks,
[Your name]
[Personal email] · [LinkedIn]
```

## 2. LinkedIn connection note (under 300 characters)

```
Hi [First name], I work in campus capital planning & facilities and I'm researching how teams build board reports and forecasts across AiM, e-Builder and Excel. Would you share 20 minutes of advice? Not selling anything.
```

## 3. Warm intro request (to someone who knows them)

```
Hi [Mutual contact],

I'm interviewing facilities and capital-planning leaders about how they build board reports and track efficiency across their systems. [Their name] at [Institution] would be a great person to learn from.

Would you be comfortable introducing us? Here's a short note you can forward:

"[Your name] works in campus capital planning and is researching how facilities teams handle reporting and forecasting. They're asking for 20 minutes of advice, not selling anything."

Thank you!
[Your name]
```

## 4. Follow-up 1 (4–5 days later, same thread)

```
Hi [First name], just bringing this back to the top of your inbox. Even 15 minutes would help a lot, and I'm flexible on timing. If someone else on your team owns capital planning or board reporting, I'd be grateful for a pointer to them.
```

## 5. Follow-up 2 (final, 5 days later)

```
Hi [First name], I'll leave it here so I don't crowd your inbox. If reporting or forecasting ever becomes a topic you'd like to compare notes on, I'd be glad to talk. Thanks for all you do for your campus.
```

## 6. Confirmation (when they say yes)

```
Thank you, [First name]! Here are a few times that work for me: [3 options with time zone]. Or pick any 20-minute slot here: [scheduling link].

To make the most of your time, I'll ask about:
- how your last board report or capital request came together
- where your data lives and where numbers disagree
- what you'd most like to see faster

No preparation needed.
```

## 7. Thank-you (same day, after the interview)

```
Thank you for your time today, [First name]. [One specific thing you learned from them] was really helpful.

As promised, here's [what you offered: a summary of what other campuses are saying / the prototype link]. If anyone else on your team or at a peer institution would be good to talk to, I'd be grateful for an introduction.

[Your name]
```

## Where to find people
- **APPA** and regional chapters (CAPPA, ERAPPA, MAPPA, PCAPPA, RMA, SRAPPA): conference speaker lists and committee members
- **Public board packets**: capital plans and deferred-maintenance presentations name the presenters
- **LinkedIn searches**: "Director of Capital Planning", "Facilities Planning", "AVP Facilities", "Space and Capital Planning", "Facilities Information Systems"
- **User communities**: AiM's Higher Ed User Group, e-Builder user groups
- **Warm intros** from every person you interview ("Who else should I talk to?")

## Targets for the first 20
- 6 research universities (R1/R2), 8 other 4-year colleges, 4 two-year colleges, 2 K-12 districts or health systems
- Roles: 8 facilities directors/AVPs, 8 capital planners or analysts, 4 budget/finance leads
- Expect roughly a 20–30% reply rate on cold email. Send about 60–80 personalized emails to land 20 calls *(assumption)*.
