# Market sizing (updated 2026-10-08 with sourced counts)

**What changed:** the institution counts now come from public sources. **Prices are still assumptions.** I found no public contract that states an annual price for facilities analytics or capital-planning software (vendors quote privately, by square footage and modules). Interviews must confirm prices. With real counts, the US market is **smaller than the first estimate**: about **$280M a year** at today's assumed prices, not $0.5–1B.

## The budget we replace (evidence)
- **Condition assessments:** consultant guidance puts a standard assessment at about **$0.25–0.35 per sq ft** (range $0.08–1.50). For UMass's 27.6M sq ft, that's roughly **$7–10M per assessment cycle**. UMass also contracts Gordian for an annual deferred-maintenance assessment.
- **The problem is growing:** Gordian's 2026 report puts deferred capital renewal at **$156 per gross sq ft** in 2025 (up 8% in a year). Campuses spend only **73.5%** of what's needed to stop the backlog growing. Gordian's database covers 43,000 buildings and 1.1B gross sq ft.
- **Analyst time:** 2–3 staff spending a quarter of their year on reporting is roughly $75–150k of salary per institution *(assumption)*.

## What this means
1. **Higher ed alone is about $117M a year.** That supports a strong, focused company ($10–30M revenue) but is thin for venture capital on its own.
2. **The venture story depends on price and expansion.** Each lever moves the total a lot:
   - doubling the average price (more modules, systems with many campuses) takes the US market to about $560M
   - adding cities and Canada adds more
3. **Validate price first.** It's the most uncertain and most important number. Ask every interviewee what they pay today for condition assessments, Gordian or AkitaBox, and reporting consultants.
