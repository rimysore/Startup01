## Notes
- **Conference shortcut:** APPA 2026 and CAPPA 2026 had sessions on exactly our problem ("Integrating Capital and Maintenance Priorities", "Capital Planning and Forecasting", "Rebuilding a CMMS to Drive Data"). Speakers on those topics are warm prospects; mention their session.
- **Avoid IU and IU-adjacent contacts** (for example, people who present alongside IU staff) until your employer conversation is done.
- Track sends and replies in a simple sheet: name, date sent, follow-up 1, follow-up 2, reply, interview date, notes.
