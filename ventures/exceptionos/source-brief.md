# **1. Product Thesis**

  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **ONE-LINE VALUE PROPOSITION\
  **We help manufacturing purchasing teams identify which supplier delivery exceptions will actually hurt operations, investigate the downstream impact, and drive the exception to resolution before it becomes a shortage, expedite, or missed customer commitment.
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The initial wedge is NOT a generic AI procurement assistant. It is a supplier-delivery exception workflow focused on changes to promised dates, quantities, acknowledgements, backorders, partial shipments, substitutions, and related supply risk.

Long-term vision: an operational Exception Management OS that connects supplier, purchase order, inventory, work order, finished product, customer order, and revenue context so a company can answer: "Something changed---what does it affect, what should we do, and did we resolve it?"

## **What the MVP should eventually do**

-   Ingest supplier communications and open-PO data.

-   Detect genuine discrepancies or missing commitments.

-   Prioritize exceptions by business impact---not merely dollar value of the PO.

-   Collect evidence across ERP/email/documents.

-   Recommend next actions and draft supplier/internal communications.

-   Track ownership, escalation, decision, and resolution.

-   Learn historical supplier behavior and successful resolution patterns.

## **What it is NOT**

-   A ChatGPT-style procurement chatbot.

-   A generic email summarizer.

-   A replacement ERP.

-   A fully autonomous purchasing system on day one.

-   A promise that AI will eliminate buyers or planners.

-   A product that modifies commercial terms or sends consequential messages without configured approval.

## **Negative ICP / Exclusions**

-   Very small shops (\<25--50 employees) unless unusually complex/high-volume purchasing.

-   Fortune 500 / global enterprises as first customers if procurement requires long RFP/security cycles.

-   Pure software/services companies with little physical supply chain.

-   Companies with fewer than \~50 meaningful supplier POs per month and little exception burden.

-   Organizations where supplier acknowledgements are already near-fully automated and exception investigation is demonstrably low effort.

-   Consultants, recruiters, ERP vendors, or competitors posing as end customers.

-   Highly regulated aerospace/defense programs requiring controlled-data access before we have security maturity.

# **6. Outreach Policy and Guardrails**

-   Use low-volume, high-relevance outreach. Prioritize learning over blasting lists.

-   Identify the outreach truthfully. Do not impersonate the founder, an employee, a customer, or a human if the system is acting autonomously.

-   Do not claim existing customers, ROI, integrations, pilots, savings, or product capabilities that do not exist.

-   Do not use deceptive "Re:" subject lines, fake referrals, fake urgency, or fabricated familiarity.

-   Use business contact information for relevant B2B outreach only. Respect applicable law, platform terms, suppression lists, and opt-out requests.

-   Every commercial email should include a simple opt-out path and the sender/business identification required by the sending jurisdiction/tool.

-   Immediately suppress anyone who opts out or asks not to be contacted.

-   Do not send sensitive/confidential information into external AI tools.

-   Do not autonomously agree to pricing, security terms, legal terms, pilots, integrations, or data access. Escalate those to the founder.

-   Never treat silence as positive feedback. Only log feedback that the prospect actually provided.

  **Reply type**                      **Agent action**
  ----------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Interested / open to call           Thank them, propose available scheduling mechanism, collect role/context, alert founder. Do not over-pitch.

  We already use X                    Ask what X handles well and where humans still investigate/resolve exceptions. Competitor data is valuable.

  Wrong person                        Thank them and ask for correct role/contact only if appropriate.

  How much?                           State that product is in discovery/pre-MVP; pricing is not finalized. Ask about current exception volume/cost and alert founder.

  Can you integrate with ERP X?       Do not promise. Say ERP X is within the systems being evaluated; ask what data/workflow they would need and alert founder.

# **9. Discovery Interview Script**

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **CORE RULE\
  **Ask about the LAST REAL INCIDENT. Do not ask "Would you use AI for this?" Past behavior is more valuable than hypothetical enthusiasm.
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.  Tell me about the last supplier delivery change, late PO, short shipment, or acknowledgment mismatch that caused real work.

2.  How did your team first learn about it?

3.  What did you do next, step by step?

4.  Which systems, spreadsheets, inboxes, portals, or people did you check?

5.  How did you determine whether the exception would actually affect production or a customer commitment?

6.  Who became involved before it was resolved?

7.  Roughly how much time did the team spend?

8.  Did it create expediting, downtime, rescheduling, excess inventory, late delivery, or customer escalation?

9.  How frequently do similar exceptions occur?

10. What does your ERP handle automatically, and what still requires humans?

11. Do you maintain an open-PO / shortage / supplier follow-up spreadsheet? Who owns it?

12. How do you prioritize 50 exceptions when only five actually matter?

13. What supplier performance data do you trust today?

14. What have you already tried to improve this workflow?

15. Why did that solution not eliminate the manual work?

16. If this problem disappeared tomorrow, what measurable outcome would improve?

17. Who would own the budget for solving it?

18. Would you be willing to show an anonymized example of the inputs and steps?

19. Would you consider being a design partner if we manually demonstrated the workflow first?

20. Who else should we speak with?

# **11. Design-Partner Qualification**

A design partner should meet at least 5 of the 7 conditions below:

-   At least \~50 meaningful supplier/order exceptions per month OR a smaller number with very high operational impact.

-   At least 2--3 people touch the workflow.

-   Current workflow crosses ERP plus email/spreadsheet/portal/human follow-up.

-   Can provide anonymized historical examples or a safe sandbox/export.

-   Can identify a measurable baseline: hours, expedite spend, shortage events, late deliveries, or resolution time.

-   Has an internal champion willing to meet weekly for 4--8 weeks.

-   Has plausible path to paid pilot if the concierge/MVP demonstrates value.
