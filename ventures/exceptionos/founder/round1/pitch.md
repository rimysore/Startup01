ExceptionOS (early-stage startup, product in development).
It reads your open-PO export and supplier emails/acknowledgements, flags real changes (new promised dates, short quantities, missing acknowledgements, backorders), and ranks them by what they will actually hit: which work orders and customer orders are at risk. Each morning your team gets a short ranked list with the evidence and a drafted supplier follow-up to approve. Nothing is sent without approval.
Start: a free 6-week design-partner test using exports and forwarded emails (no ERP integration needed), with anonymised data if you prefer.
After that: a paid pilot at $2,000/month for one plant, month to month.
