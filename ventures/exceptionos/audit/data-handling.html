What we don't need, so please leave it out

  • Prices, unit costs, PO values, payment terms or margins
  • Supplier or customer contacts, emails, phone numbers, addresses
  • Drawings, specs, routings, labour, employees or anything about people
  • Email, supplier portals, or any write access to anything

Strip it before it leaves: sanitize.py

A short, readable script you run on your own machine before sending. It turns customer, supplier, part, job, PO and order numbers into codes like SUP-5540B63F using a secret phrase you choose and keep. The files still join, so the audit works the same, but the codes can't be turned back into names without your secret. It also drops descriptions and rounds order values. It can write a code-to-name table that stays with you, so you can read our report against your real data.

Option A: you send sanitized files

  • How it reaches us: a link from your own SharePoint/OneDrive/Box that you control and can expire, or an encrypted zip with the password sent separately.
  • Where it lives: one encrypted laptop (full-disk encryption), not a shared cloud drive. Not uploaded to any service.
  • Who sees it: only [founder name].
  • What runs on it: the audit script, read-only and offline. Nothing is written back to your systems; we have no way to.
  • Deletion: files and working copies deleted within 30 days of delivering the report, or sooner if you ask; we confirm in writing.
  • What we keep: nothing from your data. With your OK, only anonymous counts (e.g. "11 jobs found"), never your name.

Option B: nothing leaves your building

You run the audit script yourself, on your machine, and send us only the one-page report (or not even that: we walk through it together on a screen-share). The script is plain Python you can read before running.

What we will never do

  • Use your data to train AI models, or send it to an AI service
  • Combine it with another company's data, or sell or share it
  • Name you as a customer or reference without written permission
  • Contact your suppliers or customers
