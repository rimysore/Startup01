How this was worked out

For each purchased part, on-hand stock and open PO receipts were netted against open job requirements in date order, once on PO due dates and once on supplier promise dates. The table lists job lines that are covered on due dates but short on promise dates. Ranked by jobs starting in the next 3 weeks, then days short, then order value. 19 hidden job lines in total, top 10 shown; 4 more are already short in a due-date view (all in findings.csv). Also: 3 releases due in the next 14 days have no supplier acknowledgement at all.

Limits, said plainly

Uses only the exports provided. Doesn't yet see allocations, substitutes, safety stock, partial receipts in transit, or multi-level sub-assemblies; your planners will know some of these. "Hidden" means hidden from a due-date view, not from your team: that's what the last column is for. Processed read-only and offline; no AI model saw the data.
