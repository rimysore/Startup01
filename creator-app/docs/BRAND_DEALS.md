# Brand deals & collabs — plan

Goal: Mitra becomes where creators *earn*, not just where they learn. Built in three phases so each one
works before the next exists.

## Phase 1 — Deal-ready (no brands needed; build first)
- **Media kit**: a public page per creator (`/kit/@handle`) generated from *verified* YouTube Analytics:
  subscribers, avg views, audience cities/age/gender, top videos, engagement. "Verified by Mitra via YouTube"
  is the selling point: brands distrust screenshots.
- **Rate card**: suggested price range per deliverable (integration, dedicated video, Short, story),
  computed from the creator's own average views × a CPM range by niche, shown as a range with the
  assumptions. Creators edit it.
- **Pitch writer**: AI drafts a short pitch to a brand (English/Hindi/regional), using the media kit.
- **Deal tracker**: pitched → negotiating → contract → content due → posted → invoiced → paid, with due-date
  reminders.
- **Invoices**: PDF invoice with optional GST; payment status.
- **Contract checklist**: deliverables, usage rights & duration, exclusivity, payment terms, revisions,
  disclosure. ASCI influencer guidelines require a clear disclosure label (#ad / #collab / "paid partnership").
  Tax on brand payments (TDS) — show a "talk to a CA" note, don't give tax advice.

## Phase 2 — Collabs between Mitra creators (network effect)
- Opt-in collab profile: language, niche, city, size, what they want (guest, challenge, cross-promo).
- Match score: same language/region, complementary niches, similar size band, audience overlap low.
- AI suggests 3 collab ideas per match; in-app "intro" request, both must accept before contact details are
  shared. No contact data shown without consent.

## Phase 3 — Brand marketplace (two-sided)
- Brands (regional D2C, apps, local businesses, agencies) post campaigns: language, region, niche, budget.
- Creators apply with their verified media kit; brands shortlist.
- Payments: escrow via Razorpay Route (needs a registered business); release on delivery; Mitra fee 10–15%.
- Brand-side plan: free to post, paid for search/filters and verified analytics.

## Start manually (concierge) before building Phase 3
1. Founder sources 10–20 small brands (regional D2C on Instagram, local apps, coaching institutes).
2. Match them by hand to founding creators; run deals over WhatsApp/email; Mitra takes a small fee or none.
3. Learn pricing, objections and contract terms; build only what was repeated.

## Revenue
Creator subscriptions + marketplace take rate + brand subscriptions. The marketplace is also the strongest
retention: nobody cancels the tool their income comes through.
