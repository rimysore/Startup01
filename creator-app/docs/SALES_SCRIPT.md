# Outbound script: the design-partner invitation

One rule above all: **it's an invitation to help build something, not an ad.** We ask for their judgment, we
give real value first, and everything we say is true: they were hand-picked from their channel, the 100 free-for-
life spots are real, and the founder really reads every reply.

**Never say:** "Congratulations, you've been selected!", "limited time", "100% guaranteed growth", fake numbers,
or anything about their channel we haven't actually seen. Never send to people who said stop.

## The shape of every conversation

1. **Show you watched.** One specific detail from their latest video. This is the whole difference from spam.
2. **Why them.** The honest reason their channel stood out (language, niche, how they talk to viewers).
3. **The ask.** "Be one of a small group of design partners: use Mitra free, tell me what's useful, and we build
   what you ask for."
4. **Make it safe.** Free, no card, no contract, they can walk away; they talk to the founder, not a bot.
5. **One small next step.** Reply "yes", or a 10-minute call. Never three asks at once.

## 3. Call script (10 minutes, when they say yes or ask questions)

**Open (30 s):** "Thanks for taking the call. I'm not here to sell you anything today. I want to understand how you
run your channel, show you one thing Mitra found about it, and see if you'd like to help shape it. Okay?"

**Listen first (4 min), and write their words down; they become the roadmap:**
- "How do you decide what to make next?"
- "What's the most frustrating part of YouTube for you right now?"
- "When a video doesn't do well, how do you figure out why?"
- "Have you tried any tools for this? What did you like or hate?"
- "If someone could take one job off your plate every week, which one?"

**Show, don't tell (3 min):** share your screen on *their* channel: the outlier score on their videos ("this one
did 3.1× your usual, here's why"), one title from Title Studio, the Monday WhatsApp report. Tie each to what they
just told you: "You said you guess what to make next; this is the 'what to make next' part."

**The ask (1 min):** "We're keeping this to a small group of design partners. Free for life for you. In return, I'll
message you every couple of weeks asking what's working and what isn't, and your requests go to the top of our
list. Want to be one?"

**Close (1 min):** set them up on the call: sign in, connect YouTube, send JOIN on WhatsApp. "Your first Monday
report arrives this Monday. I'll check in next week to hear what you think."

### Honest answers to common questions

| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "What's the catch?" | "There isn't one for you. We need real creators to tell us what's useful before we charge anyone. Your feedback is the price." |
| "Is my channel data safe?" | "We only get read-only access; we can't post or delete anything. Only you see your dashboard; our AI and hosting providers process data only to answer you (see the privacy policy). You can disconnect in one click and we delete it." |
| "I don't have time." | "That's exactly who it's for. Most of it comes to you on WhatsApp once a week. Two minutes to read." |
| "I already use VidIQ / TubeBuddy." | "Keep them! Mitra is different: it speaks your language, reads your own analytics, and tells you what to do on WhatsApp. Try both and tell me which helps more." |
| "How does it make money?" | "Later, creators who join after the first 100 pay ₹99 a month. Design partners never pay." |
| "Will it grow my channel?" | "I can't promise numbers, and anyone who does is lying. It shows you what's working and what to try; you decide." |

> Hi Ravi, just floating this up in case it got buried. Happy to set you up in 5 minutes on a call, or not at

**Day 8, last one, with value up front:**

(Only use a real observation from their channel. If you don't have one, skip the second follow-up.)

## Tracking (a simple sheet is enough)

Name · channel · language · date contacted · channel detail used · replied? · call? · joined? · their top
request. Aim for about 20 personal messages a day, and watch your reply rate: if it drops, the openings have
stopped being specific enough.
